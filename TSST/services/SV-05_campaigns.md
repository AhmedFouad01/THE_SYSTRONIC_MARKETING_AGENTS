# SV-05 Campaigns · v0.1 SKELETON · 2026-10-08

**What it is:** a campaign that holds several ads. For each ad we decide whether it sits **inside** the content plan or **outside** it, and its creative type: video, image, reel, story or carousel (user definition 2026-10-08).
**Variants:** inside the content plan · standalone (launch, offer, season) · always-on.

## Client deliverables
- Campaign brief.
- The big idea + angles per funnel stage.
- Ad matrix: ad × format × placement × angle × in/out of the content plan.
- Copy per ad (AR/EN).
- Creative assets.
- Structure, targeting and budget split, if we run media.
- Measurement plan + KPIs.
- Test plan.
- Report template.

## Blocking intake questions
1. Objective (awareness / leads / sales) and the single KPI that decides success.
2. The offer and product, and the proof we are allowed to claim. **Supplements: health claims must follow platform ad policies and local rules.**
3. Audience, platforms, dates, budget, and who runs the media. Media buying needs explicit user authority.
4. Inside or outside the content plan? Which assets already exist?
5. Tracking status (pixel / CAPI / UTMs). Without it, results cannot be read.
6. Approver and approval steps.

## Quality gates
- Every ad is native to its placement (safe zones, length, ratio).
- No unapproved claims.
- Tracking verified before spend.
- Each ad maps to one angle and one KPI.
- Creative passes the FINAL_ASSET_GATE.

## Common mistakes
- SPARK before LENS.
- One creative for every placement.
- Health or results claims in supplement ads.
- Launching without working tracking.
- A test plan with no decision rule.

## From research (GEM-SV-003, Lead review 2026-10-08: generic defaults, UNVALIDATED)
- Brief fields: objective + KPI, audience, constraints (budget, timeline, brand), key message. Creative brief fields: action, persona, hook (first 3 s), value, formats/specs, CTA.
- Creative mix by funnel stage:
  - Awareness: short video, 10–15 s.
  - Consideration: 15–30 s video, carousel, testimonials.
  - Sales: offer, UGC, catalogue.
- Inside vs outside the content plan:
  - Inside: organic posts, boosted posts and Spark/partnership ads, used for brand building and social proof.
  - Outside: dark posts, used for testing many hooks without cluttering the feed.
- Testing: one variable at a time (TikTok split test ✓ verified page). Set a decision rule before launch.
- Report: summary vs goal → KPI dashboard → creative winners/losers → audience → next tests. Weekly pacing, monthly strategy.
- **Supplement ad rules: VERIFIED by SPARK 2026-10-08 on live official pages** → `TSST/services/verified/SUPPLEMENTS_AD_POLICY.md` (33 rows + checklist). Lead spot-check: the PROMAT site exists (promat.edaegypt.gov.eg login) and the EDA 2026 ads FAQ PDF opens.
- **Hard gates before any supplement ad (Egypt):**
  1. Each product is EDA-registered.
  2. Each creative has EDA prior approval via PROMAT.
  3. Audience is 18+ for weight/muscle/health claims (Meta, TikTok).
  4. No cure/treat/prevent claims and no before/after for food supplements (TikTok Egypt group).
  5. No fat-pinching or exaggerated outcomes or timeframes (Meta).
  6. Weight-product before/after is treated as prohibited until Meta clarifies.
- Still NOT CONFIRMED: EDA claim-assessment rules ("white lists"), the legal basis and sanctions, TikTok Shop rules for Egypt.
