## 2. Pre-launch compliance checklist (supplement ads)

Nothing goes live until every line is ticked and the owner signs. Readiness stays NOT_READY.

Egypt approval (gate zero)
- [ ] Each SKU is EDA-registered as a food supplement (registration number on file). (E8)
- [ ] Each ad creative and copy submitted on PROMAT; approval or notification saved per ad. (E1-E3)
- [ ] Published content identical to approved content, with publishing pages/accounts declared (EDA statement came from a search lead; confirm on the EDA site).
- [ ] Claims checked against EDA's published claim-assessment rules (find and read them first). (E4)

Claims
- [ ] No claim to cure, heal, treat, prevent or eliminate disease; use the strictest rule (TikTok Egypt group). (M3, T6)
- [ ] No "product alone" results, easy/guaranteed results, or exaggerated timeframes. (M4, T3)
- [ ] Any results timeframe is realistic and qualified; diet/exercise context stated. (M2, T3)
- [ ] No Rx-equivalence claims; no ingredient on Google's unapproved list (ephedra, hCG, DHEA etc.). (G2, G4)

Imagery and tone
- [ ] No before/after of any body (Meta unresolved, TikTok Egypt group bans for supplements). (M7, T6)
- [ ] No fat pinching or body-part close-ups. (M6)
- [ ] No body shaming, ideal-body language, or looks-determine-confidence messaging. (M5, T4)

Targeting
- [ ] Age 18+ on Meta and TikTok for every supplement ad set, set explicitly. (M1, T1, T5)
- [ ] Spark/partnership/creator ads also set 18+ and carry the same claims review.
- [ ] Geo = Egypt only unless other markets verified separately.

Landing page (Shopify)
- [ ] Page claims match approved ad claims exactly; no extra health claims, testimonials or before/after. (T4 covers landing pages)
- [ ] Product pages, reviews, blog, bundle names checked for disease/weight-loss claims.
- [ ] Ingredients, dosage, warnings and registration number visible.

Platform account
- [ ] TikTok: approval/licensing documents ready for review; confirm with TikTok rep if rejected. (T5, T7)
- [ ] Google: no certification prompt; unapproved list check done before any Search campaign. (G3, G4)
- [ ] Policy pages re-opened within 7 days of launch (Meta change log Jul 22, 2026; TikTok pages Sep 2026).
