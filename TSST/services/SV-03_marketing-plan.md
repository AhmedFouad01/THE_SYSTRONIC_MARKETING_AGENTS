# SV-03 Marketing plan · v0.1 SKELETON · 2026-10-08

**What it is:** a short-term or long-term marketing plan that sets out the marketing stages, the funnels, and **our approach to getting the client results** (user definition 2026-10-08). Market research (SV-02) comes first.
**Variants:** short-term (launch / 90 days) · long-term (year).

## Client deliverables
**House format:** the one-page Systronic **Marketing Plan canvas** (`TSST/brands/t-sag/assets/IDENTITY.md`).
- Six numbered blocks: objectives by stage → targeting → channels → tactics table (task | channels | type | status | deadline) → radial calendar → reporting.
- A bottom row of five boxes: audience · persona/need · competition · products/services · brand persona.
- The full plan document expands the canvas.

The plan document: objectives, KPIs, stages/phases, funnels per segment, channel roles, our approach, budget frame, timeline, measurement, risks. Format: OPEN.

## Blocking intake questions
1. Business objective and target numbers (or "set baselines first").
2. Is the SV-02 research done? If not, what light scan replaces it?
3. Budget range, team/client capacity, time horizon.
4. Assets in place: identity (SV-01), website/store (SV-06), tracking.
