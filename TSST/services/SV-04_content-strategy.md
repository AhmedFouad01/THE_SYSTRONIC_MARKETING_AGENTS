# SV-04 Content strategy + content plan · v0.1 SKELETON · 2026-10-08

**What it is:** a content strategy (pillars, formats, voice, channels) and the content plan (calendar) built on the marketing plan (SV-03). Campaign ads (SV-05) may live inside the plan or outside it.
**Variants:** strategy only · strategy + monthly plan · ongoing monthly plans.

## Client deliverables
Pillars and their goals, the format mix per platform (post/carousel/reel/story), voice notes (AR/EN), a calendar with briefs per post, production specs, and the KPIs per pillar. Format: OPEN.

## Blocking intake questions
1. Do the plan (SV-03) and identity (SV-01) exist? Which goals and segments are we serving?
2. Platforms, posting capacity, and production means (photos, video, AI generation).
3. Assets available (real photos, products, people) and what is off-limits.
4. Who approves posts, and how far ahead.
