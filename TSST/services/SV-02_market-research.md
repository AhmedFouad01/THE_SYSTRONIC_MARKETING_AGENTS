# SV-02 Market research · v0.1 SKELETON · 2026-10-08

**What it is:** research done before the marketing plan (user definition 2026-10-08): market, competitors, customers and the evidence for positioning.
**Variants:** quick scan (inputs for one decision) · full study.

## Client deliverables
**House format:** the Systronic **Market Research canvas**, plus the **Business Model Canvas** where the business model is in question (`TSST/brands/t-sag/approved/IDENTITY.md`). The report expands them.

Research report: questions, method, findings graded by evidence, competitor map, customer segments/ICP/JTBD, implications for the plan. Format: OPEN.

## Blocking intake questions
1. Which decision will this research feed?
2. Market, geography, category, competitors already known.
3. What data the client already has (sales, CRM, reviews), and its privacy level.
4. Depth, deadline and budget for any paid tools or interviews.

Every claim has a source and a grade (SK-002). "Not confirmed" is used instead of guessing. The Lead opens 8+ sources (lesson GEM-CR-003: profile URLs and invented counts).
