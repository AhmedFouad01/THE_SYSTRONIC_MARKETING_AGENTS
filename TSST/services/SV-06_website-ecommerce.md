# SV-06 Website + e-commerce · v0.1 SKELETON · 2026-10-08

**What it is:** website design and development on WordPress or Shopify, plus full **e-commerce management** covering marketing and Shopify operations (user definition 2026-10-08).
**Variants:** design + build (WordPress / Shopify) · store setup · ongoing store management.

## Client deliverables
Sitemap, wireframes, UI design, built site/store (theme or custom), content and copy, apps and integrations, tracking (pixel/CAPI/GA4), launch checklist, handover/training. Management adds: catalogue ops, merchandising, CRO, reporting. Format: OPEN.

## Blocking intake questions
1. WordPress or Shopify, and why? Existing domain, hosting, store?
2. Catalogue size, payment and shipping (Egypt), languages (AR/EN, RTL).
3. Identity ready (SV-01)? Copy and photos ready?
4. Integrations: CRM, WhatsApp, ERP, tracking.
5. Who manages the store after launch?
