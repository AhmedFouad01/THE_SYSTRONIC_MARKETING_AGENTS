## Plan wording
Keep the items/versions decision path; `POST /decisions`, `/decisions/batch` (legacy), `/api/decision`,
`PATCH /job/items` return 410; drop the `decision_history` trigger; `loadWorld` side effects moved to explicit POSTs.

no writes (test on a read-only connection).

## Facts gathered (read at `next` = `2e36225`; file:line may drift, check again when designing)

### Two decision paths today
- **Old:** table `decision_history` (append-only, keys are file paths or card anchors), view `portal.decisions`
  (latest row per brand, job, key, track: `migrations/003_dash.sql:32-42`).
- **New:** `items`, `item_versions`, `item_decisions`, view `current_item_decisions` (`migrations/027_job_model.sql`).
- **The bridge:** trigger `decision_history_item_bridge` (027:157) → `portal.bridge_item_decision()` (027:127-156).
  On every insert into `decision_history` it finds the item with that key and, when the version can be decided,
  inserts the `item_decisions` row, sets `live_version` on a client approval after a team approval, and on a team
  decision resets `withdrawn` / `sent_version` and releases the job's approved items (`hold_until_all`).
- **No code inserts into `item_decisions` directly.** `decideItem` (`src/service/dash/job-model.ts:184`) validates,
  inserts a `decision_history` row (`:221-224`) and relies on the trigger for everything else.

### Who reads the old table
- `loadWorld` builds the team and client decision maps from `portal.decisions` (`jobs.ts:315`): they feed the
  deliverables of a job and the first values of its posts; the post status the UI shows comes from the item.
- `historyOf` (`jobs.ts:744`): the job's history list (last 500 rows of `decision_history`).
- `packages.ts:424`, `:460`: edit credits are counted from client `changes_requested` rows of `decision_history`;
  `:547`: the first team decision date of a client.
- `team-read.ts:117`: the team's feedback list = client `changes_requested` rows of `portal.decisions`.
- `review-provider.ts:10` (`/api/tree`, old board), `review-mirror.ts:115` and the export CLI (the PC pull task).
- `item_decisions` is read by `reports.ts:108` and, through `current_item_decisions`, by `modelItems`
  (`job-model.ts:79-84`) and `items.ts:333`, `:596`, `:626`.

## Open design questions
1. After the change, does `decideItem` still write a `decision_history` row (readers stay as they are, the trigger
   goes, one writer), or do all readers move to `item_decisions`? The first is the smaller, safer step.
2. Where does the sync of Drive posts and files go when a read may not write: an explicit `POST` the page calls when
   it opens a job, a call on the write paths only, or a timer? What does a client see for a new Drive file then?
3. Which writes on a read are allowed to stay (session touch, activity log), and how the "read-only connection" test
   says so.
4. `PATCH /job/items` also sets "shown to the client" and the client title: what replaces those when it answers 410.
5. The old board at `/legacy` becomes read-only when `/api/decision` answers 410 (it is removed in S2-07).
