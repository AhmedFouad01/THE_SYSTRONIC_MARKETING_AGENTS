## Findings and fixes
1. Settings › People & access at 1440: a person row puts the avatar alone at the start, the name and sub line at the far
   end and the role chip + ⋯ on a second line. Wanted (mockup 02 line 588): one row = avatar, then name + sub line next
   to it, then chip and ⋯ at the end.
2. Settings › Language & branding: the fields do not line up (Logo sits lower and narrower than Display name; Header
   image is indented and centred; the Header style fold is centred). Wanted: a two-column `form-grid`, every field
   starts at its column's start edge, pickers the same width as text fields.
3. Plan board: the chip "Closed · hidden from client" and the tag "We are here" overflow their card / column head.
   Wanted: they wrap or shrink inside (the tag may drop under the stage name; the chip may wrap to two lines).
4. Overview › This month's package: four meters in one cramped row. Wanted: two by two (mockup 01 `.pk-lines`), one
   column on the phone.
5. Clients cards: the name sits at a different height from card to card (the cards stretch and their content spreads).
   Wanted: content starts at the top in the same order on every card; the foot (package + Open) sticks to the bottom.
6. Health "waiting": `clientReviewFacts` counts items of delivered jobs and of projects hidden from the client
   (`client_visible` false), which the client cannot see or decide. Wanted: neither counts as waiting on the client,
   in the health word, the job words and the attention rows alike (one shared read). Tests for both cases.
7. "Waiting on client · today" for an item sent a day ago: check which timestamp the waiting age reads
   (`sent_at ?? created_at`) and where `sent_at` comes from; the age must run from when the version was sent to the
   client (the team's approval of that version). Fix if it reads something else; if it is right and only the preview's
   backdating missed the real column, say so and change nothing.
