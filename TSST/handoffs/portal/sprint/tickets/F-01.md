"المشكلة دي من زمان ومتعالجتش اللوجين بتاع صفحة العميل بايظ UI". Gate: none to build; the deploy needs his OK.
Diagnosis: a five-reader workflow with a skeptic per area (2026-11-11); the digest is in the sprint session's scratchpad

## Causes (each confirmed by a second reader in the code; the logo also measured on live)
1. **The tall empty block.** The workspace's light logo stored on live is a 1281×1281 canvas whose wordmark fills only
   1106×76 in the middle. `.auth__logo` sizes the image by width (240px) with `max-block-size: none`
   (`web/src/components/ds/system.css:477-479`), so the box is 240×240 of mostly empty space (dark logo 512×47 → 240×22,
   which is why dark theme and the mock look right). `normalizeLogoBytes` (`src/service/dash/branding.ts:58-73`) never
   trims transparent margins. The page also jumps on load (default logo first, stored logo after the fetch). The team
   phone top bar shows the same image about 17px wide.
2. **The floating language button.** `AuthLayout.tsx:13` puts it first inside `.auth__brand` (a 240px stretch column);
   the button is `fit-content`, so it sits alone at the start edge. Added by `8ace63b` (2026-10-27) with no layout rule.
3. **"by THE SYSTRONIC" far below.** Right-aligned under the wordmark by design (`system.css:485`); it only looks
   detached because of cause 1.
4. **Glued words.** `8ace63b` replaced whole sentences by `text("…")` + value: "Client portal ·Yakebda" and
   "Try signing in again in60s." (`ClientSignIn.tsx:44,50,51,64,89`); the same in `ClientJobCards.tsx:23` ("Review3
   files") and `ds/Roadmap.tsx:50,64,78`.
5. **Footer.** On client pages the two footer parts are joined with the word "and" (`WorkspaceBrand.tsx:41`, `BidiList`).
6. **Narrow submit button** on the team login, invite and PIN cards (fit-content since `5cc517e`; the client card
   stretches it through `client-profile.css:79`).
Not defects: the client banner (148px) and the "Client portal · name" label are the approved R93 design
(`docs/r91/CX-3-sign-in-logo.md`); they stay.

## Fix
1. Web guard: `.auth__logo` gets a height cap (64px), `object-fit: contain`, centred, and reserves its height so the card
   does not jump when the stored logo arrives.
2. Server: workspace **header logos only** (light and dark) are trimmed of fully transparent margins before the resize
   (`sharp` trim on the alpha channel; a logo with an opaque background is left as it is). Client logos, the company
   document logo, avatars and thumbnails are not trimmed. Test: a square transparent-padded PNG comes back tight; an
   opaque square comes back unchanged.
3. The stored live logo: fixed by Ahmed re-uploading in Settings › Workspace after the deploy (the trim then applies),
   or by uploading a tight crop now. No direct database write.
4. The language control leaves `.auth__brand`: its own row at the card's top inline-end corner, above the logo, with
   the `order` the branded client card needs (`client-profile.css:76-77`); 44px on the phone; no overlap at 390.
5. Sentences with placeholders (`formatText`): "Client portal · {0}", "Try signing in again in {0}s.", "Try again in
   {0}s."; the same for `ClientJobCards.tsx:23` and `Roadmap.tsx:50,64,78`; EN + AR keys.
6. Footer on client pages: the two parts joined by " · ", each in `<bdi dir="auto">`.
7. The submit button stretches on every auth card (one rule on `.auth__card .form`, the client-only rule removed).

   stored logo loads.
