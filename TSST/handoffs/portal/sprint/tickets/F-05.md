## What is true today (read in the code by two readers)
1. **Overview shows the plan as one chip.** The Direction card (`ClientOverviewTab.tsx:49-60`) draws one stage chip,
   one campaign link, "n of m done" and the next milestone. Three of the five Overview blocks are about review states.
   The five-stage board lives only on the Plan tab ("Growth stages").
2. **Two rules for "where we are".** Overview: the first open project with a goal, by order, dates ignored
   (`overview.direction.stage`). Board: by dates (`hereStage`, `lib/plan.ts`). They can disagree (Overview "No
   campaign yet" while the board marks a stage).
3. **A campaign with two stages fills one column.** `boardColumns` puts a campaign under its first goal only
   (`lib/plan.ts` `campaignColumn`), and the card shows no stage chips. Written in S3-04 on purpose; the server lists
   the campaign under every goal (`plan.stages[i].campaigns`), and the client Plan page (F-04) already draws it so
   (`clientBoardColumns`).
4. **A client's posts have no home.** Before S3 the page had "<client> posts": every post of every job in one list
   with search, open and New post (`v-before-sprint:web/src/components/ClientPostsList.tsx`). S3-04 removed it; the
   ticket said posts go "to the Work page", which has no post rows. `PostsList` (`components/ds/PostsList.tsx`) and
   `api.getBrandPosts` are still in the code with no caller; `OLD_VIEWS.posts` redirects to the Plan tab. The server
   still sends `upcoming_posts` on the client read; nothing draws it.
5. **Brand-level and job-level milestones cannot be edited, ticked or deleted anywhere.** The Plan tab's milestones
   card lists only the campaign in view (read-only, "edit on the campaign page") or, with no campaign, the brand's own
   with "add" only (`ClientPlanTab.tsx` `MilestonesCard`). Yet "Needs attention" counts overdue milestones of every
   level and links there. `MilestoneModal` (`MilestoneEditor.tsx:13`) already edits and deletes a milestone of any
   scope.

## Fix
A. **Overview, Direction card: the five stages at a glance.** A compact strip of the five stages in order with their
   names; the current stage marked "We are here" by the BOARD's rule (`hereStage`), so the two screens never disagree;
   under each stage the names of its campaigns (a campaign under every stage it has), a quiet dash when none. The
   existing lines (campaign, milestones, next) stay under it; the "Plan" link stays. The strip loads the plan
   (`api.getMarketingPlan`) and never blocks the rest of the Overview (its own loading and error line). Phone: the
   strip wraps or stacks without horizontal scroll.
B. **Board: a campaign under every stage it has**, on the team Plan tab, from `plan.stages` as the server gives them;
   when a campaign has more than one stage its card shows its stage chips. Reverses one line of S3-04 with Ahmed's OK.
   The shared `StageBoard` and the client Plan page keep working (one rule for both sides where possible).
C. **Posts get a home: a "Posts" tab** on the client page (Overview / Plan / Posts / Settings): every post of the
   client's jobs in one list with search, the job name, open (lands on the job with the item selected) and "New
   post" (asks for the job when the client has more than one running job), reusing `PostsList` and
   `api.getBrandPosts`. Old `?view=posts` links land on it. On Overview, each running job shows its next posts
   (thumbnail, platforms, publish date) from `upcoming_posts`, with a link to the Posts tab.
D. **Milestones editable again.** The Plan tab's milestones card lists, in groups, the campaign's milestones, the
   client's own (brand level) and the jobs' milestones, each row with tick done / undo, edit and delete through
   `MilestoneModal`; "Add milestone" is always offered (scope picked in the dialog). The campaign page keeps its own
   editor. An overdue milestone in "Needs attention" opens the Plan tab at this card.
E. Words in `web/src/copy.ts` and the Arabic twin; mock data so the vite mock shows each case (a campaign with two
   stages, brand-level and job-level milestones with one overdue, posts in two jobs).

Not in this ticket (recorded): the roadmap calendar (S6-01, mockups not approved); the old Campaigns strip, ads counts
and dates on board cards; reorder of projects; the first-tab layout of mockup 02 beyond the Direction card.
