## Design
1. Server: `GET clients` adds `stage: FunnelStage | null` per brand, from the same rule as `direction.stage` (export the
   campaign choice from `client-overview.ts` as one function and call it from both; one projects query for all brands,
   not one per brand). Test in `tests/v1-clients-health.test.ts` or a new file; a line in `docs/DASH-API.md`.
2. Card (`Clients.tsx`), top to bottom:
   - the logo well as today, tinted edge / top band in the stage tint when a stage exists (`data-stage` on the card; a
     4px band in `--sy-stage-<stage>`), then the name (logo as title stays);
   - three facts in one row: **health word** as a chip: On track (`chip--neutral`… use cyan-tinted `chip--progress`),
     Waiting on client (`chip--waiting-client`), Needs attention (`chip--changes`); **stage** chip in the stage tint
     ("—" when none); **waiting count** "n waiting on client" (`health.waiting`; hidden at 0);
   - under them one muted line with the first reason when the word is attention: Changes owed · Stuck with the client ·
     Payment overdue · Milestone overdue · Over allowance;
   - foot: package name or "No package" (muted, one line) and "Open".
   Gone: the `UsageRing`, the credits lines, Projects / People / Last activity facts, the "Active" badge.
3. Toolbar: search + `All | Active | No package` (Active = `active_jobs > 0`; No package = no package); the header meta
   line (active / invited people) goes.
4. Order: attention first, then waiting on client, then on track; by name inside a group.
5. Phone (390): one column, the three facts wrap, 44px tap target (the whole card is the link).
6. Copy `copy.clients.*` / `copy.th.clients.*` EN + AR: على المسار · مستني العميل · محتاج انتباه · n مستني العميل ·
   من غير باقة · افتح · reasons: تعديلات مطلوبة · واقف عند العميل · دفعة متأخرة · محطة متأخرة · فوق حد الباقة.
7. Mock: `/clients` rows carry `stage` (Nour Studio activation, Alpha Gym acquisition, Cairo Dental revenue per
   SAMPLE-STATE § 2). Tests: pure helpers (`web/src/lib/clients.ts`: sort, filter, chip class, reason line) in `.mjs`;
   AR words asserted.
