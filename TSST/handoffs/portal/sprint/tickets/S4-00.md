## Plan wording
One maker per item (also before anything is uploaded); per client: a reviewer, then the approver (the client's
approver, or a team member); decisions recorded by step and by person; the maker cannot decide on their own item;
"Planned" until the maker sends.

## What this ticket delivers (said plainly)
- Every item can have one named maker. An item the maker has not sent is **Planned**: in nobody's review list.
- Per client, the owner sets a **reviewer** (a team member) and may name the client's **approver** (a client person).
  The order is: maker sends, reviewer decides, approver decides.
- A decision row says which step it was and who made it (a user id or a client person id, not only a name).
- The maker, and the person who sent the version, cannot decide it. The server refuses it.
- One read model, `turnsOf`, says for every item "which step, with whom, since when". Home, Tasks, the Job page and the
  Review queues (S4-01, 03, 04) read it and nothing else.
- **A client with no reviewer set behaves exactly as today.** The new behaviour is switched on per client by that field.
- Not here: the screens (S4-01, 03, 04), Tasks (S4-08), the Schedule gate and its exception (S4-03 enforces them; this
  ticket gives the facts), the in-house workspace (postponed; the columns allow it with no second migration of these
  tables), notifications (nothing here sends anyone anything).
- **No real client is switched on before S4-04 (Review queues) ships.** Until then the old queue shows every item.

## 2. The model

### 2.1 Words
- **Maker**: the one team member who owns making the item. Nullable.
- **Submitter**: the user who sent the current version for review (stored on the item).
- **Reviewer**: per client, one team member; **approver**: per client, who decides the second step. Core: the client's
  approver. In-house (later): a team member.
- **Step** of a decision: `STEP_OF_TRACK` in code: track `team` = `review`, track `client` = `approval`. `track` keeps
  meaning "the lane"; it is not renamed. In-house later: the approval step stays the `client` lane, made by a team user;
  the person columns say who. **There is no `step` column.**
- **Ordered client**: `client_settings.reviewer_user_id` is not null. All enforcement is for ordered clients only.
- **Active** (one helper, `src/service/dash/active.ts`, used by the read model, the routes and the settings validation):
  a user is active when `disabled = false AND deleted_at IS NULL`; a client person is active when
  `status <> 'removed' AND deleted_at IS NULL`, and an approver when also `role = 'approver'`.

### 2.3 The item's step machine
`ordered` = the client's `reviewer_user_id` is set. For the **current version** `v` of a live item (not deleted; job not
archived or deleted), `T` = the team decision on `v`, `C` = the client decision on `v`. `submitted(v)` =
`ordered ? submitted_version = v : true` (an unordered client has no Planned state: as today).

The first matching row is the step. "Post lives" are those of 1.1.

| # | Condition | Step | Status word (SPEC section 2; no new word but Planned) | With whom | Who may act |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `live_version = v` | `ready` when the item is a post (organic or ad) whose life is `draft`, **or an ad whose life is `rejected`** (it goes back to the maker); **`done` for every other life** (`scheduled`, `posted`, and the ads `in_review`, `active`, `paused`, `completed`) and for every non-post item | Approved / Scheduled / Posted / Delivered | `ready`: the maker (to schedule). `done`: nobody | `ready`: schedule (S4-03 gates it, 2.8) |
| 2 | not `submitted(v)` | `planned` | Planned | the maker | the maker, the submitter-to-be (anyone when the maker is null), or an owner: **submit** |
| 3 | `T` null or `pending` | `review` | Pending review | the effective reviewer (2.6) | an eligible reviewer: decide. The maker or an owner: **recall** while the version has no team row or only `pending` rows. After a team approval the way back is **withdraw** (row 4), not recall |
| 4 | `T` is `approved` and `withdrawn` | `review` (flag `withdrawn`) | Pending review | the effective reviewer | an eligible reviewer re-decides (an approval ends the withdrawal and releases again, as today) |
| 5 | `T` is `changes_requested` | `changes`, source `review` | Changes requested | the maker | the maker: a new version, then submit. The eligible reviewer may change their own decision on the same version (as today) |
| 6 | `T` is `approved`, not sent (held by `hold_until_all`) | `held` | Pending review (held) | **`with` = the first blocker's own `with`** (blockers sorted by version `created_at`, then key); `with_all` = the distinct `with` of all blockers; `blocking = [{ item_id, title, step, with }]`. A blocker at `planned` or `changes` is with its maker (the owners group when it has none); at `review` with its reviewer; at `approval` with the approver. A Planned item **is** a blocker (`job-model.ts:164`) | an owner may release early (`release-early`, unchanged); the blocking items act in their own steps |
| 7 | sent and `C` null or `pending` | `approval` | Waiting on client (approver is a client) / Waiting on approval (approver is a team member, later) | the approver (2.6) | an approver: decide (client route) |
| 8 | sent and `C` is `changes_requested` | `changes`, source `approval` | Changes requested | the maker | the maker: a new version, then submit. **The approver may decide again on the same version** (approve), as today |

Rules:
- A version has one step. One item is counted once everywhere.
- **Planned is a state of the current version.** A new version (file bytes changed, a Dash edit) has no submission, so
  for an ordered client it is Planned again, whatever the previous version did. The Drive sync makes versions by itself;
  it must never put work before a reviewer without the maker's act (question 4).
- **Edited after approval** (computed flag `edited_after_approval` on the turn): life is `scheduled` or `posted`,
  `live_version` is not null and `current_version > live_version`. No block in S4-00; S4-03 decides what to do.
- A team approval still releases the item to the client in the same transaction (`releaseItems`): "reviewer approves" and
  "the item reaches the approver" are one act, as today. The meaning of `sent_version`, `live_version`, `withdrawn`,
  `hold_until_all` is unchanged. The only new state is `submitted_*`.

Transitions (inside `decideItem` and the three new writers, under the job's lock):

| Action | From step | To step | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| submit | planned | review | `submitted_version = v`, `submitted_at = now`, `submitted_by_user_id = actor`; event `submitted`. When the maker is null the actor becomes the maker (event `maker_changed`, from null) |
| recall | review (no team row, or only `pending` rows, on `v`) | planned | the three `submitted_*` columns to NULL; event `recalled` |
| review: approve | review, held-release | approval or held | decision (team lane), then `releaseItems` as today |
| review: request changes / `pending` | review, approval, changes(review) | changes (or review for `pending`) | decision; `sent_version = NULL` as today |
| approval: approve | approval, changes(approval) | ready or done | decision (client lane); `live_version = v` as today |
| approval: request changes | approval, ready (until locked) | changes | decision (note required) |
| withdraw (unchanged route) | approval | review (withdrawn) | as today |
| new version | any | planned | `saveItem` as today; `submitted_*` are left and no longer match `current_version` |

### 2.4 "Planned" and the accepted decisions per step
Planned is not a stored word; it is row 2, computed. "Wrong step": for an **ordered** client, a decision outside the
step it belongs to is refused. For an unordered client nothing is refused (as today).

| Step | Team-lane decision (any status, `pending` included) | Client-lane decision |
|---|---|---|
| planned | refused 409 `not_submitted` | refused 404 (not sent, as today) |
| review (and withdrawn) | accepted from an eligible reviewer (2.6) | 404 (not sent) |
| changes (review) | accepted from an eligible reviewer (changing their mind) | 404 (not sent) |
| held | accepted from an eligible reviewer | 404 (not sent) |
| approval | accepted from an eligible reviewer only to take it back (`changes_requested` or `pending`); a repeated approval is a no-op | accepted from an approver |
| changes (approval) | as `approval` | an approver may approve again (as today) |
| ready, done | `live_version = v`: read-only (409 `conflict`, as today); a repeated approval returns the existing row | an approver may take an approval back until the life is locked (409 `approval_locked`, as today) |

A team `pending` row is a decision: it takes the version back from the client (`job-model.ts:245`), so the maker rule and
the turn rule apply to it as to any status.

| `by` | the name shown, as today | as it was |

### 2.6 How the next person is computed (the "with whom" for each state)
- **Effective reviewer** of an item (client order + item maker `M` + submitter `S` of the current version):
  1. unordered client: `with` = the earliest-created active owner; **eligible** = every active team member (as today).
  2. ordered: `eligible` = ({the named reviewer if active} plus every active owner) minus {`M`, `S`}. `with` = the named
     reviewer when eligible, else the earliest eligible owner. **An owner may always act as reviewer for that client
     (the reviewer is away); the decision is recorded `stand_in = true` when the named reviewer was active and was not the
     decider; never on their own item** (the minus). The named reviewer who is the maker is out of the set, so the
     owners decide.
  3. `eligible` empty and the team has exactly one active member: that member (the maker) is eligible, row
     `self_decided = true`.
  4. `eligible` empty otherwise: `with` = the owners group (below), `blocked = 'no_eligible_reviewer'`. Fix: name another reviewer, hand the
     item over (an owner changes the maker) or have another owner decide. Reported to the owner by the read model.
- **Effective maker** (steps planned, changes, ready): the maker if active. Maker null: `with` = the owners group,
  `unassigned = true` (the normal case for items the Drive sync makes). Maker inactive: `with` = the earliest active owner, `maker_inactive = true` (any owner may act).
- **Approver** (step approval): core: the named client person when set and active (`approver_person_id`), else
  `{ kind: 'client', name: <client display name> }`. **The name is a label**: every active client approver and every old
  link session of the client may still decide (question 1). In-house later: `with` = `approver_user_id`.
- **The owners group** (no turn is left without a name): `with = { kind: 'group', name: 'owners', id: <the earliest active owner> }`. It is used for `unassigned` and for `blocked`, and every owner counts it in their needs-action rows (5.2).
- **Held**: 2.3 row 6.
- `since`: planned = the version's `created_at`; review = `items.submitted_at` (no events join; for an unordered client,
  the version's `created_at`, as the queue sorts today); changes = the decision's `at`; approval = the version's
  `sent_at`; ready = the client decision's `at`.
- `next_with` (before the act, for the confirm sheet of Decision 14): `next: { approve, changes }`, each a `with` value:
  at review, approve = the approver (or the blockers when the job holds), changes = the maker; at approval, approve =
  the maker (`ready`, to schedule), changes = the maker.

### 2.7 The rule "the maker cannot decide on their own item"
Enforced in `decideItem`, under the job lock, for an **ordered** client and the `team` lane. The refused actors are the
item's **maker** and the **submitter of the current version**, compared by user id, never by name. So changing the maker
or having someone else press send does not open the rule.

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| The actor is the maker or the submitter, others are eligible | 403 `maker_cannot_decide` |
| The named reviewer is the maker or the submitter | out of the eligible set; owners decide (stand-in) |
| The owner is maker and the only candidate, other members exist | 403 `maker_cannot_decide`; the item is `blocked` (2.6.4) |
| One-person team (one active member, the maker) | allowed, `self_decided = true` |
| Maker deactivated | cannot sign in; the item is with an owner (`maker_inactive`); an owner reassigns |
| Item without a maker | no maker to exclude; the submitter rule still holds; `unassigned` is shown |
| The eligible set is empty and the actor is not the maker or the submitter | 409 `no_eligible_reviewer` (checked **before** `not_your_turn`; otherwise it could never fire) |
| The actor is not in a non-empty eligible set | 403 `not_your_turn` |
| **Switch-on marks** (3.3): a version whose `submitted_version` mark has `submitted_by_user_id IS NULL` | **grandfathered**: neither the maker nor the submitter rule applies to that version. A new version, or a real submit, ends it |
| Unordered client | none of this applies |

**Who may change the maker** (`PUT /items/:id/maker`): an owner (to anyone active, or to nobody); the current maker (to
another active user); any team member for themselves when the maker is null. **Only an owner may clear a maker.** Every
change writes an `item_events` row `maker_changed` (`by_user_id` = the actor, `from_user_id`, `to_user_id`).

### 2.8 Facts for scheduling (used by S4-03, not enforced here)
`scheduleGate(turn)` returns `{ allowed, reason }`: allowed for an unordered client (as today) or at step `ready`.
**Where it goes (S4-03):** the `life` field of `POST /posts` (`publishing.ts:391`) and of `PATCH /posts/:id`
(`:499`, next to the `posted` check at `:572`). `PATCH /items/:id` is not one (1.1). The exception is an `item_events`
row `schedule_exception` with a required note (allowed by 036).

### 3.3 The moment the owner sets the first reviewer (the switch-on)
Runs in the transaction of `PUT /client/settings` that sets `reviewer_user_id` **and only when
`client_settings.review_switched_at IS NULL`**; it then sets `review_switched_at`. Under every job lock of the client,
locked one at a time in sorted job order. A later clear-then-set changes no data (marks stay, the field changes only).
For each live item whose current version has no mark:
- **Mark only on evidence of review**: the current version has a team or client decision, or `sent_version = v`, or
  `live_version = v`. "Has a file" and "life is not draft" are **not** reasons. No evidence: stays Planned.
- The mark is `submitted_version = v`, `submitted_by_user_id = NULL` (a system mark, grandfathered, 2.7),
  `submitted_at` = **the earliest evidence time** (the first decision's `at` or the `sent` event's `at` on `v`), not now,
  so ages are kept; an `item_events` row `submitted` with the same `at` and the note `review order switched on`.

**Dry run** (read-only, before the `PUT`): `GET /api/v1/client/review-order/preview?brand=&reviewer_user_id=` (owner)
answers `{ first_time, steps: { planned, review, approval, changes, held, ready, done }, would_mark, maker_is_reviewer:
[{ item_id, title, job, step }], no_maker: n }`: the counts per step as they would be after the switch, and the items
whose maker is the chosen reviewer (they would go to the owners). The owner sees it, then saves.

Already with the client: step `approval`, unchanged. Approved before: `ready` or `done`. Client "changes requested": step
`changes`. Clearing the reviewer later: marks stay, the client behaves as today again, nothing is lost.

### 3.4 Makers on new work
`POST /posts` and `POST /items` pass `c.user.id` as the maker (`publishing.ts:461`, `work.ts:326` already have `c.user`).
**The maker is set only when the item row is created; an edit (`PATCH /posts/:id`, `PATCH /items/:id`) or a sync never
overwrites it** (`syncPostItem` called from `PATCH /posts/:id` at `:612` passes no maker). **The plan import is not changed:** it
makes posts only (1.6); their items come from the sync, so their maker is the job's `default_maker_user_id` or null (the
owner sets many at once with `PUT /job/maker`, `apply_to_unassigned`). The job default applies **only to items the Drive
sync makes**. A restored item keeps its maker (1.6). A maker change is an event (2.7).

## 5. Read model: who must act
One module `src/service/dash/turn.ts`: a pure core and one loader.

### 5.1 Inputs
```
turnsOf(db, world, { brand?, job?, itemIds?, today, includeDone? }) -> ItemTurn[]
turnOf(item, ctx) -> ItemTurn      // pure
```
`ctx` per brand in scope (one query set): the review columns of `client_settings`, the users (id, name, role, active), the
active client approver people, per job `archived` / `deleted` / `delivered`, per project `archived` / hidden (the facts of
`clientReviewFacts`), and per item the client's last decision across versions (for `owed_since`).

### 5.2 Output
```
ItemTurn {
  item_id, brand, job, key, title, type, version, publish_on, life,
  ordered,
  step: 'planned'|'review'|'approval'|'changes'|'ready'|'held'|'done',
  changes_source: 'review'|'approval'|null, withdrawn: boolean,
  status,                                   // the SPEC words
  with: { kind: 'user'|'client_person'|'client'|'group', id, name, inactive? },   // never null: 'group' = the owners
  with_all: With[], blocking: [{ item_id, title, step, with }],     // held only
  eligible: { users: string[], client: 'approvers'|'person'|null },
  next: { approve: With|null, changes: With|null },
  since: ISO|null, days,
  maker: { id, name, active } | null,
  submitted_at, submitted_by: { id, name } | null, grandfathered: boolean,
  client_decision: { status, by, at } | null,       // the last client decision on the version in view
  owed_since: ISO|null, owed_days: number|null,     // health's rule, below

  blocked: 'no_eligible_reviewer'|null, unassigned, maker_inactive,
  self_review_allowed, stand_in_possible
}
```
Helpers (pure): `everyone(turns)` groups the turns by `with` (user, client person, client) for the owner's Everyone view
and the person filter, with no viewer needed; `needsAction(turns, userId)` = `with` is that user, or `with.kind = 'group'` and the user is an active owner (the `unassigned` and `blocked` items count under the owners; P1 still holds: every row has a name);
`waitingOnOthers(turns, userId)` = the user is the item's maker or the submitter, or is the client's **named reviewer by
the client's current order** (not by old decision rows, whose `by_user_id` is NULL), the item's `with` is someone else,
and the step is `review`, `approval`, `held` or (for the reviewer) `changes`; `jobRollup(turns)` = one word per job, the
step with the highest priority `changes > review > approval > held > ready > planned > done`, with the counts per step and
the distinct `with` (default; S4-01 may change it from the drawing); `scheduleGate` (2.8). `done` rows come only with
`includeDone`.

### 5.3 Health and "waiting": one definition, no double count
- A version has one step. Planned, review, changes and held items are never "waiting on the client" (already so today:
  they are not `itemVisible`).
- **`waiting` (health) = the turns at step `approval` whose `with.kind` is `client` or `client_person`**, in a job that is
  not delivered, not in an archived project, not in a hidden project (F-07). A team-member approver (later) reads
  "waiting on approval" and is not counted as waiting on the client.
- **`owed_since` = health's rule exactly**: the client's last `changes_requested` (any version) that no newer SENT version
  has answered (`sent_version IS NULL OR sent_version <= d.version`), dropped for a delivered job and an archived project;
  **a hidden project keeps it** (F-07). It is independent of the step. `owed_days` = `daysSince(owed_since)`. Health's
  `changes_owed` reads `owed_days`.
- **Mandatory parity fixture:** "version 1 changes requested by the client, version 2 uploaded, not sent". Health says
  owed; the turn is `planned` (ordered) or `review` (unordered) with `owed_since` set. Others: hidden project (owed kept,
  waiting null), archived project (both null), delivered job (both null), withdrawn item (left out).
- S4-00 leaves `clientReviewFacts` as it is and adds the parity test (for an unordered and an ordered client the number of
  `waiting` facts equals the `approval` turns, and `owed` agrees with `owed_since`). S4-01 moves health onto `turnsOf`.
- Home's "Needs action" is `needsAction`; "Needs your review" is `needsAction` with step `review`. A row needs a named
  `with`; `unassigned` and `blocked` rows come with their flags so S4-01 can group them (question 8).

## 7. Options rejected
| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| A list of approvers per item | The brief and Decision 18 make the order per client. A per-item list is rows for every item of every client, hard to see and to change. | A `step` column that replaces `track` | Touches every reader of both decision tables and the client payloads. |
| Backfilling `by_user_id` on old decisions | Append-only table; a name is not an identity. |
| Planned as a stored status | Two places would have to agree; a new version would need a reset write. Computed cannot drift. |
| Enforce for every client at once | A client with items in flight and a maker guessed from a log would block on deploy day. Per-client switch-on keeps "nothing that works today may stop". |
| The sync auto-submits a new version | The sync makes versions by itself; sending is the maker's act. |
| A SQL trigger to refuse the maker | S2-03 chose one writer; the rule needs the client's order and the user list. |
