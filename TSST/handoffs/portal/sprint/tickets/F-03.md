# F-03: reviewing takes fewer taps and less scrolling (client and team)

Live fix after the deploy. Reported by Ahmed on 2026-11-11, right after F-02 went live: "حتى المراجعة متعبة" (even the
review is tiring). Gate: none to build; the deploy needs his OK after he sees it. Diagnosis: workflow
`dash-client-page-poor-diagnosis` (reader + skeptic on the review flow; digest `poor-diagnosis.txt` in the sprint
session's scratchpad). Branch `sprint/f-03-review-flow` from `origin/next` (`c7d4859`), worktree
`~/Work/tsst-portal-f03`. Web only. No route, no migration.

## Causes (read in the code by two readers; sizes computed from the CSS, not yet measured)
1. **One item at a time, by hand.** After a decision the client stays on the decided item; there is no next / previous
   and no move to the next item (`ClientReviewQueue.tsx`); the same for the team (`JobModelItems.tsx`). Older than the
   sprint.
2. **The buttons are at the bottom of a long item, never sticky.** On the phone the decision buttons are
   `position: static` (`styles/job-model.css:215-224`). F-02 put above them: the version pills, the media uncropped up
   to `min(70vh, 640px)`, every caption in full and a files list that, for a post, repeats the media already on the
   stage. On a phone that is about two to three screens per post where it was one.
3. **No answer after Approve.** `reload()` of `useResource` returns nothing, so `await reloadAll()` does not wait; the
   item keeps its old state with live buttons until two list requests answer (`ClientReviewQueue.tsx:56,68,170`).
4. **The Versions row is drawn for one version too**, above the media, and arrives late (the picture jumps down).
5. **A carousel needs one tap per picture** on small arrows; no swipe.
6. **768 to 1024px:** one column, but the list / detail swap applies only under 768, so the item opens under the whole
   list with no scroll to it. On desktop the detail panel is not sticky.
7. **Team:** "Open item" on the Review queue leaves for the Job page, whose back link never returns to Review; the
   queue then forgets its tab and filters. The panel selection snaps back to the item in the URL
   (`JobModelItems.tsx:41-56`). The client nav badge does not refresh after a decision.

## Fix
Client (`ClientReviewQueue.tsx`, `ReviewItemView.tsx`, CSS, `copy.ts` + `copy/ar/copy.ts`):
A. **Move on after a decision.** After Approve and after a sent revision request: one status line that names the item
   ("Approved: title" / "Request sent: title") with a "Back to it" action when the item can still be opened; the next
   item of the queue is selected and the panel scrolls to its top; when the queue is empty, the list shows its
   "all caught up" state. Buttons stay busy until the lists have reloaded (make `reload` awaitable).
B. **Next / previous** beside "Item 3 of 12", in the order of the list, 44px on the phone.
C. **Decision bar sticky on the phone** (under 768): Approve and Request changes side by side in a bar fixed to the
   bottom of the screen with the surface background and the safe-area padding, shown while an item that can be decided
   is open; the reason box of "Request changes" opens above the bar and takes focus; nothing is hidden behind the bar
   (bottom padding). When the viewer cannot decide (preview, viewer role, decided item) there is no bar.
D. **Shorter item, nothing hidden.** For a post, "Files of vN" no longer repeats the media that is on the stage: one
   "Open original" link under the stage for the media on screen, and the list shows only files that are not on the
   stage (hidden when none). The Versions row shows only when the item has more than one version and reserves no
   height before that. On the phone the stage is capped at `min(60vh, 520px)`, still uncropped. The copy stays in
   full (F-02's rule).
E. **Swipe a carousel** on touch (scroll-snap track or pointer swipe); the count and the arrows follow.
F. **768 to 1024px** uses the same list / detail swap as the phone (or scrolls the opened item into view); on desktop
   the detail panel sticks under the top bar when it is shorter than the screen (no rule change for other users of
   `.side-panel` / `.workspace-grid` unless stated).
G. List rows show a two-line excerpt of the copy (as the team rows do). The nav badge refreshes after a decision.

Team (`JobModelItems.tsx`, `Job.tsx`, `Review.tsx`):
H. After Approve / Return for edits, the next "Needs action" item of the job is selected, with a status line.
I. Next / previous in the item panel; the selection no longer snaps back to `?item=` after it was applied once.
J. The decision bar is sticky on the phone, as for the client.
K. "Open item" from the Review queue: the Job page's back link returns to the Review queue, which keeps its tab and
   filters (in the URL).

Not in this ticket (recorded): reviewing in place in a drawer on the Review queue (larger; a proposal for S4-04);
approving from a list row; clamping the copy (against F-02); a video poster.
