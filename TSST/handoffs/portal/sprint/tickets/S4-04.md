## 3. The read model
### 3.1 Route: new, additive: `GET /api/v1/review/queues` (team)
Why not parameters on `GET /review`: its answer is a flat array whose shape, order and `queue` values are pinned for unordered clients by the S4-00 / S4-01 recorded transcript and read by five tests and the mock; a reader-relative answer with counts, client list and paging does not fit an array. The old route stays byte for byte; the unordered branch of both routes calls ONE helper (the old `queueOf` + filter, moved out of the route, no change of behaviour) so they cannot drift. The old route is removed in a later cleanup, not here.
Query: `tab` = `needs_you` | `waiting` (default `needs_you`), `brand`, `project_id`, `output` (as today's validation), `limit` (default 50, max 200), `offset`. Bad value = 422.
Answer: `{ counts:{needs_you,waiting}, tab, total, rows:[Row], clients:[{brand,brand_name,stage}], projects:[{id,name,brand}] }`.
`counts` and the two lists are for the reader and ignore the filters (so the options never vanish); `total` is the tab with the filters. `Row` = `item_id, brand, brand_name, job, job_title, key, project_id, project_name, title, output, preview, files, maker, turn` (the whole `ItemTurn` with `viewer`) and `selectable: boolean`. No client session can call it (access `team`, the client walk stays green).

### 3.2 The two queues, from the reader's side (`me` = the signed-in user, any role)
| | Client WITH a review order | Client WITHOUT one |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs your review** | `turn.step = review`, `turn.with` is me (a user, or the owners group when I am an active owner: the blocked case), and I am not kept out. A member who is the maker never sees their own item here (the verdict keeps them out), except the one-person team, who may decide their own (`self_review_allowed`, recorded). Held, Planned, changes, approval: never. | The old `needs_team_review` rows, **for every active team member** (nobody is a maker or reviewer there; any member may decide today: `reviewerSide` unordered branch, `turn.ts:244-247`). |
| **Waiting on others** | `waitingOnOthers(turns, me)` as it is (`turn.ts:729`): I am maker, submitter or the named reviewer; someone else holds; step review, approval, held; changes only for the reviewer. | The old `waiting_for_client` rows, for every active member; `with` = the client (or its named approver). |
Member, named reviewer and owner use the same two rules. A client that has no order and one that has are mixed in one answer, per client. The owner's stand-in power (an owner may decide an item whose named reviewer is someone else) does not put the item in their tab; they find it on Home (Everyone) or on the job page (Q2). Home's Mine for an unordered client stays "earliest owner" (S4-01); this page keeps the old "every member" for it, as today: a difference by design, listed in the parity test.

### 3.3 Selectable, sort, paging
`selectable = tab is needs_you and turn.step = review and turn.viewer.decide = null`; a blocked row is shown with the reason and is not selectable. Sort: client groups by their oldest waiting item first (ties by client name), inside a client by `publish_on` ascending (nulls last, P6), then `turn.since` ascending, then `item_id`; `waiting` the same. `offset` paging over this one in-memory order (the turns are already loaded): after a decision the page re-reads from `offset 0` with the size it had. The count is never the length of a page.

### 3.4 Cost and proof of the counters
- Queries: the same world load as `GET /review` + `turnsOf`'s five; no query per item; `withTurns` is given the known turns. The page makes ONE call per view (counts inside) instead of two 200-row calls. PR a measures it on the seeded database for 1 and 8 items (today `GET /review` = 22, `tickets/S4-00.md` build record); stage adds at most 1 or is left out.
- **Rule that keeps today's clients matching Home:** for a client without an order, `tab rows(needs_you)` = `GET /review?queue=needs_team_review&brand=X` rows and `tab rows(waiting)` = `...waiting_for_client`, for every user; and Σ over those clients of `counts.needs_you` = Home `kpis.pending_review` = Σ job-card `counts.pending` (S4-01 b parity). **For a client with an order:** Σ over active users of `needs_you(user)` + review rows whose `with` is a group + held rows = Home `pending_review` of that client. Test `tests/v1-review-queues-parity.test.ts` asserts all three on a world with both kinds of client, and that `GET /review` equals its recorded transcript.

## 4. Bulk approve (Decision 14)
- **Selectable** = `selectable` rows only (needs-your-review tab; filters apply; "Select all" = the selectable rows loaded; a refused-by-rule row has no check).
- **Confirm sheet** as drawn: "Approve n items?", the review line when the selection is one ordered client, one row per item "Title · vN" + "to {next} for approval", grouped under the client name when the selection spans clients. `{next}` = `turn.next.approve`: the named approver, the client's name for a client without a person, and for a held item (job set to "hold until all") the blocker's holder with the held words of Q5. Buttons: Cancel (quiet), "Approve n items" (green: the only green).
- **Execution:** one `POST /items/:id/decisions {version: row.turn.version, status:'approved'}` per item, in order (reuse `approveItems`, `item-bulk.ts:10`); a progress line; each result kept. **Refusals** show as today's list "n of m approved. These could not be approved:" + title + the server's words (`refusalMessage`, `lib/review-order.ts:15`): 403 `maker_cannot_decide`, 403 `not_your_turn`, 409 `not_submitted`, 409 `no_eligible_reviewer`, 409 "newer version" (reload), 404 (deleted). Not atomic; a refusal never stops the rest (stated in S4-00 risks). After the run the read is repeated; a refused row stays with its check cleared.
- **Never schedules or posts:** the sheet has no schedule words and the page has no Schedule button (S4-03). The server path writes a decision and releases to the next person; PR d adds a test that after a bulk approve of posts `items.life` and the posts' own life are unchanged. No server change is needed for this rule: the ordered rule is `decisionVerdict`; the unordered client keeps "the next person is the client".
- Held: a held item is not selectable (it is already approved by the team).
