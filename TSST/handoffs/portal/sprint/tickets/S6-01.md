## 1. What is true today
- **"Work" is `web/src/pages/Jobs.tsx` (93 lines), route `/jobs`.** It is a list of JOB cards, not of items. Filters, all in the address: `q` (search over title, client name, job), `client`, `status` (job status: planned, in_progress, in_review, delivered, on_hold), `type` (job type: campaign, content, identity, research, website, plan, other), `archived=1` (`Jobs.tsx:17-21`, `:42-44`). The page reads `GET /jobs` once (`src/server/v1/work.ts:673-700`, access `team`, no pagination, no date on a card) and filters in the browser.
- **No existing read gives dated items for a month across clients.**
  - `GET /jobs` has no item dates (`cardOf`).
  - `GET /items?brand&job` is one job (`work.ts:305`).
  - `GET /home/week?view&offset&person` (`work.ts:660`, S4-07) is dated by day, but: it is seven days; a member sees only own rows (Everyone and `person` are 403 for a member, `work.ts:670`); and its "Going out" group holds only `ready` posts and `done` posts with life scheduled or posted (`src/service/dash/week.ts:172-183`), so a Planned, In review, Waiting on client or Changes requested post is not on it. It is the team's to-do view, not a calendar.
- **The facts a calendar needs already sit in one cheap read.** `turnsOf(db, world, {brand?, today, includeDone})` (`src/service/dash/turn.ts:479`) returns one `ItemTurn` per item with `publish_on`, `life`, `step`, `status` (nine words: Planned, Pending review, Waiting on client, Waiting on approval, Changes requested, Approved, Scheduled, Posted, Delivered: `turn.ts:23`), `brand`, `job`, `title`, `type`, `withdrawn`. Cost: at most five or six queries whatever the number of items, jobs or clients (comment at `turn.ts:469-478`); it already leaves out items of archived or deleted jobs and of a deleted client. Done turns come only with `includeDone: true` (`turn.ts:464`).
- **Which day an item sits on (one rule, already in code).**
  - A post: `posts.publish_on`, a `date` column (migration 016, `ALTER TABLE portal.posts ADD COLUMN publish_on date`).
  - An ad (`kind = 'ad'`): `adDayOf` = `ad_start`, else `publish_on` (`src/service/dash/ads.ts:10`; migration 026 adds `ad_start`, `ad_end` as `date`). `turnsOf` already fills `ItemTurn.publish_on` with it (`turn.ts:599-600`), as `job-word.ts:104` does.
  - Other item types (video, design, plan, website, document) have no date column: they are not on a calendar. A post with no date (every Drive-imported post: "a Drive post gets a date once it is edited in the Dash", `posts.ts:228`) is not on it either.
  - `date` columns carry no zone, so no shift can happen to a day. "Today" is the Cairo day (`src/core/day.ts`, `todayOf`; `DASH_TIME_ZONE = Africa/Cairo`).
- **Week start.** The brief and the plan speak of a `week` setting from migration 037. That setting holds the WORKING DAYS (default Saturday to Thursday) and a short name per weekday, nothing else; the week itself "always starts on Saturday: not a setting" (`src/service/dash/week-settings.ts:1-4`; `weekStart` in `reports.ts:56`; the Week uses it, `week.ts:31`). Read by `weekSettings(db)` (one query).
- **Status tints exist for words, not for a calendar.** The status words come from `ItemTurn.status`; the approved inset gives the shape only: `03-client-plan.html:506-511` (`.cal` 7 columns, gap 4; `.d` day cell min-height 64, sunk surface; `.is-today` selection fill; `.ev` chip 10px text, one line, ellipsis). The inset's text sizes (10 to 12px) are below the 14px of the kit: the fuller drawing must settle them (Q4).
- Team pages have no language switch; team words are `Localized` EN + AR pairs under `copy.th.*` in `web/src/copy.ts`, tested in a node test (S4-04 ticket, section 1).
- Not read: how `turnOf` words an AD's life (active, paused, completed, rejected) as one of the nine statuses; whether `turnsOf` returns withdrawn items (`ItemTurn.withdrawn` exists); the Job page's way to open one item by address (`pages/Job.tsx`, `lib/job-link.ts`); `cardOf` fields `type` and `status` (used for the filters, section 2.1).

## 2. Design
### 2.1 The read (new, `src/server/v1/work.ts` next to `home/week`; team; GET only)
`GET /calendar?month=YYYY-MM[&client=][&status=][&type=][&q=]`
- `month` default the current Cairo month; bad format 422; range: 24 months back to 24 forward of today, else 422.
- The window is the grid the page draws: the Saturday on or before the 1st to the Friday on or after the last day (35 or 42 days, so the grey days of the neighbour months are filled).
- Rows: every item of type `posts`, not withdrawn (default, not read: confirm), whose day (rule above) is inside the window, from ONE `turnsOf` call (`includeDone: true`) plus the world the route already loads. Row shape: `{item_id, brand, brand_name, job, job_id, title, day, kind: "post" | "ad", status, step, life, with_name: null}` (no person, no price, no caption: the calendar shows what the item is and where it stands, not who holds it; the Week does that). `job_id` is the stable job id (`world.jobProjects`), so the page links with `jobHref`.
- Answer: `{ month, today, window: {from, to}, week_start: "saturday", working_days: [6,0,1,2,3,4], rows: [...sorted by day, brand_name, title], undated: N, truncated: false }`. `undated` = the count of dated-less posts of the same filters (a number, never a list: imported posts have none, the list could be long). `working_days` from `weekSettings` (one query) so the grid can dim a non-working column as the Week does.
- **Filters are the list's filters, resolved by the same function.** `client` = brand id; `status`, `type`, `q` apply to the JOB exactly as `Jobs.tsx:42-44` does (`q` over `title brand_name brand job` lower-cased, plus the item's own title); the route takes the job set from `scanAll` + `cardOf` (as `GET /jobs`, `work.ts:690-698`) and keeps the rows of those jobs. `archived` is not a calendar filter: `turnsOf` leaves archived jobs out (Q3). Acceptance 3 pins it: the set of jobs equals the ids `GET /jobs` returns for the same query.
- Size: no pagination (the month is the page). A cap of 600 rows: over it the answer keeps the first 600 by day and says `truncated: true` (the page shows one line "More than 600 items this month: filter by client"). Query count constant, pinned by a test that seeds 300 dated items over 12 clients (the same method as S4-01's `homeFacts` count test).
- Time: dates are plain days; `today` is the Cairo day from `todayOf(world.now)`. A test with the clock at 22:30 UTC on the last day of a month (already the 1st in Cairo in summer, 01:30) and the month filter shows the item on its own `date`, and `today` on the Cairo day.
- Docs: route table regenerated (`scripts/route-table.ts --write`; the test `tests/api-docs.test.ts` fails if stale).
### 2.2 The page (web; waits for the drawing, R57)
- A control "List | Calendar" on Work (`view=calendar` in the address next to the filters; the list stays the default; the filters and the search box are the same ones and keep their values when the view changes). Month navigation: previous, next, "Today" (to the current Cairo month); `month=YYYY-MM` in the address. No drag, no create, no edit: a chip is a link to the item's job page (Q2); the page makes one `GET` per month and filter change, nothing else.
- **Desktop (768 and wider): the month grid of the inset**: seven columns, Saturday first (reads `week_start`), a header with the weekday names; non-working days dimmer by `working_days`; today with the selection fill; days of the neighbour months in the muted style. Each day shows up to 3 chips (title, tint by status, one line); more: "+N" that opens the day as the same row list (one day only, no popover that traps focus). A chip says client and title (`dir="auto"`, `<bdi>`).
- **Phone (under 768): a row list**, one group per day that has items (day heading, then rows: tint dot, title, client, status word, kind post or ad); empty days are not drawn; "Today" scrolls to today's group; 44px rows.
- Tints: one tone per status WORD through the existing status tokens (the same family the chips use), colour never the only carrier: every chip and row also holds the status word (title attribute on the grid chip, text in the list). The mapping table is in the drawing; tokens only, no hex.
- States: loading, error with retry, empty month ("No dated posts this month", with the `undated` count line), truncated line.
- Words: `copy.th.calendar` EN + AR in `copy.ts` and `copy/ar/copy.ts`; month and weekday names from `Intl` with the page's language and numbering through the existing `num` / `<bdi>` helpers.

## 4. Acceptance
1. Access: 401 with no session, 403 for a client link, 200 for a member and an owner (no owner-only part: a member sees every client, as on `GET /jobs`).
2. Each refusal gives 422 and nothing else: a bad month, a month outside 24 months, an unknown `status` or `type` value.
3. Same job set: for each of `client`, `status`, `type`, `q` and two combined, the jobs behind the rows are a subset of `GET /jobs` for the same query, and every dated post of those jobs is a row.
4. The day rule: a post is on its `publish_on`; an ad on `ad_start`, else `publish_on`; an ad with `ad_end` is on its start day only; an undated post is no row and adds to `undated`; a video, a design or any non-post item is no row; an item of an archived job, a deleted job or a deleted client is no row.
5. Window: a day on the grey part of the grid (the previous month's last Saturday) is returned; a day outside the window is not; the window is whole weeks and no longer than needed (a test per case: a 28-day February that starts on a Saturday gives 28 days; a 31-day month that starts on a Friday gives 42).
6. Cost: the query count is the same with 3 items and with 300; the answer holds no key of caption, price, person, file path or team note (a test lists the row keys).
7. `truncated`: 601 dated items give 600 rows and `truncated: true`; 600 give `false`.
8. Time: the Cairo cases of section 2.1; `week_start` is `saturday`, `working_days` equals the stored setting, default when none.
9. The calendar writes nothing: source scan, the route file and `calendar.ts` call no `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `save*` or `PATCH`; the page issues only GET calls (gate counts the calls).
10. Gate `web/qa/S6-01/gate.mjs`, 390 and 1440 (1024 and 1100 too), light and dark, EN and AR team copy, on the mock build: the control switches the view and keeps the filters; month navigation changes the address and the grid; today marked; a chip shows client and title and its status word is reachable; "+N" opens the day; phone shows the row list and no grid; the empty month and the truncated line; no sideways scroll; targets 44px under 768; text 4.5:1; no stroke; no hex colour or raw `px` radius; the Arabic words load.
11. The grid matches the approved drawing in `mockups/s6/` and the inset `03-client-plan.html:506-511` (same cell, same today fill, same chip shape); every difference is listed in the build record with the drawing's reason.
12. Older gates stay green: F-06 and S4-02 (Work and its New job), S4-07 (Week, shares `weekStart` and the working days), S4-01.
13. Checks green: root typecheck, lint, the full suite with `--no-file-parallelism`; web typecheck, `ds:check`, build, `test:api`. No migration; `git diff --stat migrations/` is empty.
