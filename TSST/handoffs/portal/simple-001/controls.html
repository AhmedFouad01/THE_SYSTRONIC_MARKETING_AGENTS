Controls & Guardrails Sheet

Core Guardrails & Policies

Guardrail 1 (Versions): Deleting a draft version is permitted, but no delete is ever allowed on the approved or posted version.
Guardrail 2 (Documents & Invoices): Read + Edit only. Cancel document instead of delete to preserve audit trail and financial history.
Client Links: Generated links must stay findable with Copy link · Renew · Revoke on every row. Expired links show Renew only.
Client Decisions: The client can change their decision until the item is scheduled or posted.
