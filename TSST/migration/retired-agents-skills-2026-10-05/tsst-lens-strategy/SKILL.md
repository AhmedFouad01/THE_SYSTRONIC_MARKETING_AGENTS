# TSST LENS Strategy

Use TSST/LENS/AGENT.md as authority.

Required behavior:
- start from the business decision and research questions;
- separate observed facts from inference;
- attach locatable sources to material claims;
- do not invent personas or customer facts;
- connect positioning claims to proof;
- connect objectives to KPI definitions;
- request CREATIVE review for visual communication and SPARK review for media/measurement claims.
