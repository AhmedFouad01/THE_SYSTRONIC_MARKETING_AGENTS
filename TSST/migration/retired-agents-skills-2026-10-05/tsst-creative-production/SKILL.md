# TSST Creative Production

Use TSST/CREATIVE/AGENT.md, CREATIVE_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md, TOOLCHAIN.md and FINAL_ASSET_GATE.md.

Rules:
- visual idea first, not copy-on-template;
- preserve brand specificity, hierarchy and focal point;
- adapt composition natively per placement;
- generated imagery/video is production material, not automatic final output;
- inspect actual renders;
- reject distorted logos/products, broken Arabic, weak hierarchy, accidental crops and uninspected exports;
- never self-grant APPROVED_FOR_RELEASE.
