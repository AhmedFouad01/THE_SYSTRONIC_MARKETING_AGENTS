# TSST-SPARK-SK-004 Evaluation — Meta Campaign & Creative Fundamentals

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Build a Meta campaign and creative test plan with three hypotheses from an evaluator-supplied strategy and measurement fixture. Do not create or launch a campaign.

## Required deliverables

- objective and optimization-event rationale;
- proposed structure, placements, audience, exclusions, and automation choices;
- CREATIVE_AG creative brief for native Reels assets;
- three-hypothesis test matrix;
- metrics, decision rules, minimum evidence, policy, and pre-launch QA.

## Test conditions

Include a weak optimization event, one horizontal silent asset, safe-zone violations, a fragmented audience proposal, and an unsupported claim.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score signal readiness, structural simplicity, native creative requirements, controlled testing, current-source use, and CREATIVE_AG handoff quality.

## Automatic failures

Launch/account mutation; unsupported claim; creative accepted without CREATIVE_AG QA; or Reels plan omits vertical/audio/safe-zone considerations.
