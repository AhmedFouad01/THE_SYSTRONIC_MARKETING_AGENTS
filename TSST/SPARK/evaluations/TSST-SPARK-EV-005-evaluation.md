# TSST-SPARK-SK-005 Evaluation — Google Search & Performance Fundamentals

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Design a Google Search structure for an evaluator-supplied demand scenario. Do not create or modify a campaign.

## Required deliverables

- intent taxonomy;
- campaign/ad-group and keyword map;
- match and negative logic;
- ad/asset-to-landing-page map;
- conversion, value, bidding, and budget rationale;
- settings, hygiene, monitoring, and search-term review plan.

## Test conditions

Include ambiguous keywords, brand and competitor intent, weak landing-page coverage, an invalid conversion action, and a deprecated bidding recommendation.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score intent reasoning, query controls, ad/landing alignment, signal readiness, maintainability, and current official-source checks.

## Automatic failures

Structure based on volume alone; invalid conversion used for bidding; deprecated feature recommended; or unsupported ad claim.
