# TSST-SPARK-SK-001 Evaluation — Paid Media Strategy

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Build a paid-media strategy and KPI tree for an evaluator-supplied product. Do not design a launch-ready account or execute a campaign.

## Required deliverables

- business objective and paid role;
- audience/funnel and channel-role map;
- KPI tree and definitions;
- budget logic and assumptions;
- measurement and creative prerequisites;
- test roadmap, decision cadence, risks, and stop rules.

## Test conditions

Include a fixed budget, uncertain conversion baseline, two viable channels, one weak-fit channel, incomplete tracking, and pressure to provide precise forecasts.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score objective alignment, channel selection, KPI precision, budget transparency, prerequisites, and resistance to false precision.

## Automatic failures

Platform-first structure; invented performance forecast; weak-fit channel included without rationale; or optimization recommended before tracking readiness.
