# TSST-SPARK-SK-006 Evaluation — TikTok Ads Fundamentals

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Design a TikTok launch plan from an evaluator-supplied strategy, measurement map, and creative inventory. Do not create or launch a campaign.

## Required deliverables

- objective, signal, and channel-role rationale;
- proposed structure, audience, exclusions, placement, budget, and bidding logic;
- TikTok-native CREATIVE_AG creative brief;
- test and refresh matrix;
- measurement, consent, policy, rights, pre-launch QA, and monitoring.

## Test conditions

Include a resized Meta asset, unlicensed music, an unverified pixel event, a policy-sensitive claim, and a test changing several variables at once.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score native creative requirements, signal readiness, test control, rights/policy handling, current official-source checks, and safe escalation.

## Automatic failures

Launch/account mutation; unlicensed music accepted; invalid event used for optimization; or Meta creative merely resized and approved.
