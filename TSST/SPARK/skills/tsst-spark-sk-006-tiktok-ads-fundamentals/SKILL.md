# TSST-SPARK-SK-006 TikTok Ads Fundamentals

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective

Create a TikTok launch plan that uses platform-native creative, a valid objective and signal, controlled testing, and explicit policy and measurement checks.

## Triggers

Use for TikTok media planning, launch-plan design, TikTok-native creative requirements, objective/targeting review, measurement readiness, or policy preflight. This skill does not authorize launch.

## Reusable principles

- Select the objective and optimization signal from the business outcome and measurement maturity.
- Treat TikTok creative as native full-screen communication, not resized Meta creative.
- Current TikTok guidance favors vertical 9:16, high-resolution content, safe-zone awareness, clear messaging, sound/voice, and regular creative refresh; test these practices rather than treating them as laws.
- Keep audience and structure broad or segmented only when the hypothesis, exclusions, and data justify it.
- Define creative and media tests together, with one main variable where practical.
- Policy, rights, consent, and event diagnostics are launch prerequisites.

## Workflow

1. Confirm objective, offer, audience evidence, market, budget, conversion event, consent, policy category, and authorized creative assets.
2. Validate pixel/events readiness and source-of-truth definitions.
3. Choose campaign objective, optimization event, structure, placements, audience logic, exclusions, and bid/budget approach with rationale.
4. Create an CREATIVE_AG brief for TikTok-native concepts covering 9:16, safe zones, sound, people/product visibility, hook, message, CTA, and rights.
5. Define test cells, hypothesis, primary variable, success metric, evidence threshold, and refresh logic.
6. Check landing experience, UTMs/naming, policy, brand safety, and account prerequisites.
7. Define diagnostics, monitoring, stop/escalation rules, and reporting limits.
8. Recheck TikTok Academy, Help Center, ad specifications, and policies before acceptance.

## Output contract

- objective, signal, and channel-role rationale;
- proposed campaign/ad-group/ad structure;
- audience, exclusion, placement, budget, and bidding logic;
- TikTok-native CREATIVE_AG creative brief;
- test and refresh matrix;
- measurement, policy, rights, and pre-launch QA;
- monitoring and escalation plan.

## Guardrails

- TikTok creative is not Meta creative resized.
- Do not launch, spend, or alter an account during bootstrap.
- Do not present best practices as guaranteed performance.
- Do not use unlicensed music or unsupported claims.
- Do not optimize on an invalid or unverified event.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Objective and optimization event fit the business outcome.
- [ ] Structure and audience logic have a testable rationale.
- [ ] Creative requirements are TikTok-native and rights-aware.
- [ ] Test variable, metric, and decision rule are explicit.
- [ ] Measurement, policy, consent, and landing checks are complete.
- [ ] Current official TikTok sources were checked.

## Practical evaluation

Design a TikTok launch plan from a supplied strategy, measurement map, and creative inventory. The fixture includes a resized Meta asset, unlicensed music, an unverified pixel event, and a policy-sensitive claim. The candidate must block those items and produce a compliant test plan without launch.

## Common failure modes

- recycled creative with no TikTok-native treatment;
- platform objective chosen before conversion readiness;
- audience complexity unsupported by data;
- sound and safe-zone requirements omitted;
- creative rights or policy ignored;
- testing plan with several uncontrolled variables.
