# TSST-SPARK-SK-004 Meta Campaign & Creative Fundamentals

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective

Create a pre-launch Meta test plan that connects business objective, valid conversion signal, campaign structure, placements, creative hypotheses, and decision rules.

## Triggers

Use for Meta campaign planning, Reels creative requirements, placement strategy, Advantage automation review, or creative A/B test design. This skill does not authorize launch.

## Reusable principles

- Select the campaign objective and optimization event from the business outcome and signal quality.
- Use automation and broad delivery only when measurement, exclusions, budget, and review conditions are understood.
- Treat placement-native creative as a design requirement, not a resized afterthought.
- For Reels, current Meta guidance emphasizes vertical 9:16 video, audio, and key messages inside safe zones.
- Tie each creative to a named hypothesis and isolate the primary variable when practical.
- Use A/B testing and predeclared decision rules rather than post-hoc winner stories.

## Workflow

1. Confirm business objective, conversion action, audience constraints, geography, budget, attribution assumptions, policy, and creative rights.
2. Check measurement readiness and block the plan if the optimization signal is invalid.
3. Choose campaign objective, optimization event, placements, audience approach, exclusions, and automation settings with rationale.
4. Define structure that preserves enough signal and budget for learning without needless fragmentation.
5. Send CREATIVE_AG a creative requirement brief covering format, 9:16 treatment, audio, safe zones, message, proof, CTA, and variants.
6. Define three creative hypotheses, controlled variables, cell allocation, duration logic, and success metrics.
7. Document pre-launch QA, policy checks, monitoring, and stop/escalation rules.
8. Recheck current Meta official guidance before acceptance.

## Output contract

- objective-to-event rationale;
- proposed campaign/ad-set/ad structure;
- placement and automation rationale;
- audience/exclusion logic;
- CREATIVE_AG creative brief and three hypotheses;
- test matrix, metrics, decision rules, and minimum evidence needs;
- measurement, policy, rights, and pre-launch QA checklist.

## Guardrails

- Do not launch, spend, or change an ad account during bootstrap.
- Do not use resized non-native creative as the sole Reels plan.
- Do not claim Meta best practices guarantee performance.
- Do not accept creative without CREATIVE_AG visual QA.
- Do not use platform-attributed results as proof of incrementality.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Objective and optimization event are valid.
- [ ] Structure avoids unsupported fragmentation.
- [ ] Placement and automation choices have rationale.
- [ ] Reels requirements include 9:16, audio, and safe zones where used.
- [ ] Three hypotheses and decision rules are explicit.
- [ ] CREATIVE_AG QA and current Meta policy/doc checks are required.

## Practical evaluation

Build a Meta test plan with three creative hypotheses from a supplied strategy and measurement fixture. Include structure, placements, Advantage choices, CREATIVE_AG requirements, test logic, and pre-launch checks. Do not create or launch a campaign.

## Common failure modes

- campaign type chosen before objective;
- too many fragmented ad sets;
- creative variants change several major variables at once;
- safe-zone/audio requirements omitted;
- automation accepted without exclusions or diagnostics;
- winner declared without sufficient evidence.
