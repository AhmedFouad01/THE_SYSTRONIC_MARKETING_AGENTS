# TSST-LENS-SK-013 Organic Social Content System & Calendar

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective

Give a brand a repeatable organic social system: pillars grounded in positioning, formats matched to platform, a realistic cadence, a calendar, clean briefs to STUDIO, reply rules, and a loop that turns metrics into the next plan.

## Triggers

Use for content pillars, monthly/quarterly social calendars, format and cadence decisions, content briefs, community-management guidelines, and organic performance reviews. Planning structure follows SK-006; copy follows SK-011; Arabic follows SK-012.

## Reusable principles

- Pillars come from positioning (SK-005): each pillar proves or expresses one message pillar for one audience need.
- Sustainable cadence beats ambitious cadence. Plan to the team's real capacity.
- Format follows platform behaviour and the job (reach, trust, consideration, retention), not trend-chasing.
- Mix: value content, proof content, brand/personality, and light conversion. Keep conversion share deliberate, not accidental.
- Organic claims are held to the same proof rules (SK-002, SK-005).
- Nothing is published by this skill; calendars are drafts for approval.

## Workflow

1. Confirm brand, positioning/message house, audience segments, goals (reach, trust, leads), languages, capacity, approval chain, and existing assets/rights.
2. Derive 3-5 pillars. For each: message pillar served, audience need, proof type available, example topics, formats, and what it must never say.
3. Pick platforms by where the audience actually is and what the team can sustain. Fewer, done well.
4. Assign formats and cadence per platform (table; verify current platform behaviour before committing).
5. Build the calendar (template below) with seasonal and commercial moments (Ramadan/Eid and others: verify dates; see SK-012).
6. Write a brief per asset in the STUDIO handoff format. Draft copy through SK-011/SK-012.
7. Set community engagement rules (below).
8. Define metrics, review rhythm, and the learning loop; log decisions.

### Formats per platform (working defaults, verify current behaviour)

| Platform | Strong formats | Typical job | Starting cadence (adjust to capacity) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Reels, carousels, Stories, single image | Reach (Reels), saves/trust (carousels), relationship (Stories) | 3-4 feed/Reels per week, Stories several times a week |
| Facebook | Short video, image posts, community/Group posts, events | Local community, older/broader audience, support | 3-5 per week |
| TikTok | Short native video, trends adapted to brand, creator-style | Reach and discovery | 3-5 per week if production allows |
| LinkedIn | Text posts, document carousels, short video, founder/team voice | B2B trust, hiring, authority | 2-4 per week |

Cadence figures are starting hypotheses, not rules; label them as such in the plan.

### Calendar template

| Date | Platform | Pillar | Format | Topic / hook | Language | CTA | Proof/asset needed | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | IG | P1 | Reel | one-line idea | AR-EG | Save | source/quote ID | name | Idea / Brief / In design / Review / Approved / Scheduled-by-human |

Keep a pillar-mix summary under the table (counts per pillar, per format, conversion share). Status "Scheduled-by-human" means a person did it; this skill never publishes.

### Brief-to-STUDIO handoff format

- Asset ID, platform, format, dimensions/ratio (verify), duration;
- goal and single message (from message house), audience;
- hook (first line / first 2 seconds) and final copy (from SK-011/012) with exact strings, language, direction, numeral style;
- required proof/claim text and qualifier;
- brand assets to use (logo, palette, fonts, product images) with rights status;
- must-include / must-avoid; accessibility (captions, alt text, contrast);
- deadline, approver, revision limit.

### Community engagement and reply guidelines

- Reply in the commenter's language and register (Arabic/Egyptian: SK-012). Target response windows are set by the brand; state them.
- Tone: helpful, human, brief. Thank, answer, then invite a next step; no defensive replies.
- Questions needing facts (price, stock, policy): answer only from approved facts; otherwise "we will check and reply" and escalate.
- Complaints: acknowledge, move to a private channel for details, never argue publicly. Hand customer-account issues to the brand's human support.
- Hide/escalate abuse, spam, and legal or safety issues per the brand's policy; log them.
- No fabricated engagement, no asking for fake reviews, no incentivised claims without disclosure.
- Sensitive topics (religion, politics, tragedy): do not engage promotionally; pause scheduled content during major sensitive events (human decision).

### Organic metrics and learning loop

- Per goal: reach/impressions, views and watch/retention (video), engagement rate, saves/shares, profile visits, follower quality, link clicks, DMs/leads. Define each metric as the platform defines it; do not compare across platforms as if equal.
- Cycle: plan -> publish (human) -> collect weekly -> review monthly: which pillar/format/hook beat the baseline, which to repeat, retire, or test.
- Change one variable per test where possible (hook, format, length, posting time). Small samples are directional only; say so (SK-002).
- Record learnings and the next-cycle decisions in the plan. Attribution and paid amplification go to SPARK_AG.

## Output contract

- pillar set with message-pillar mapping and exclusions;
- platform/format/cadence plan with capacity check;
- calendar table for the period, with pillar-mix summary;
- STUDIO briefs for scheduled assets;
- community reply guidelines and escalation list;
- metrics definitions, review rhythm, and learning log template;
- open dependencies (assets, proof, approvals, dates to verify).

## Quality gates / critical failures

- CRITICAL: calendar item with an unproven claim, unlicensed asset, or unverified date/offer.
- CRITICAL: any publish, schedule, or reply action taken by the skill; it only drafts.
- Every pillar traces to a positioning pillar.
- Cadence fits stated capacity.
- Each asset has an owner, status, and approver.
- Arabic items flagged for SK-012 native review.
- Metrics tied to goals; no vanity-only reporting.

## Handoffs

- Positioning -> SK-005. Plan structure -> SK-006. Copy -> SK-011 / SK-012. Evidence -> SK-002.
- Production of visuals/video -> STUDIO. Final visual approval -> CREATIVE_AG.
- Paid boosts, tracking, attribution -> SPARK_AG.
- Publishing and account actions -> human owner of the brand account.

## Common failure modes

- pillars invented from taste, not positioning;
- calendar that exceeds team capacity;
- same format everywhere regardless of platform;
- all promotion or no CTA at all;
- briefs missing exact copy, so STUDIO guesses;
- no review loop, so nothing is learned.
