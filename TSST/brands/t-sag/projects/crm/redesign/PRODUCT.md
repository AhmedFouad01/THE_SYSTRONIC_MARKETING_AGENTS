# PRODUCT.md (Impeccable product context, CRM)

Brand `t-sag` · project `crm` · status: proposal, NOT_READY · written 2026-10-09 by CREATIVE_AG (round C1a).

## What it is
An agency CRM with two-way ad data. Leads, contacts, deals and pipeline sit next to Meta, Google and TikTok data: spend and clicks flow in, conversions and audiences flow out. Each product is one tenant (a client workspace); the agency works across several clients.

## Users and roles
- **Agency users** see all clients or a chosen set, switch client from the top bar, and sit above the client workspace.
- **Client users** see only their own workspace.
- Roles inside a workspace: **M** (manager: edit all, bulk actions, integrations, consent), **P** (practitioner: own records, log activity), **R** (reader: read only, may see all records).
- Sensitive fields are masked without the `sensitive.read` permission.

## Surface and mode
Impeccable mode: **Operate** (app UI). Scanability, consistency and the real usage scene outrank expression. Brand lives in precise details: a blue that leads, one red used sparingly.

## Use scene
Desk work on a 1440 laptop or monitor, long sessions in office light and evening dark; quick checks on a 375 phone (lead follow-up, approve, call). Dense tables, frequent bulk actions, daily KPI glance.

## Languages and reach
- Arabic (complete RTL) and English are equally first-class. Mixed AR/EN text in names, companies and notes is normal and must never break.
- Desktop 1440 and mobile 375 for every screen.
- User-entered names are one field (`dir=auto`); UI chrome is bilingual.

## Hard product rules
- CRM numbers and platform numbers are always shown separately, never blended.
- Unknown values are labelled ("Unknown / direct"), never blank.
- A lead needs a phone or an email.
- No browser-native unstyled controls anywhere.

## What users hate today (Ahmed)
Heavy black, visible divider lines everywhere, buttons sitting on lines, a messy layout with misaligned heights.

## Anti-references
Default Chart.js charts, navy KPI slabs, boxed inner scroll frames, near-black dark mode.
