# CRM redesign: decisions (Ahmed)

The MVP was a base to see; the redesign may restructure navigation and screens.

| ID | Date | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D-01 | 2026-10-09 | A new sidebar group, **Marketing / التسويق**, holds Ads, Ads insights, Forms, SEO (confirmed 2026-10-09) and **Segments**. Segments leave the CRM group. Contacts keeps access: add a contact to a segment, show segment membership on the record, and filter by segment. |
| D-02 | 2026-10-09 | Agency staff "Login As" a client user (impersonation): **not now**. |
| D-03 | 2026-10-09 | Passwordless client sign-in (magic link): **not now**. Normal sign-in + 2FA. |
| D-04 | 2026-10-09 | **Product core** = a strong CRM plus strong marketing APIs: two-way data between the CRM and the ad channels, so ads are steered by CRM data. The navigation and the way the CRM works follow a detailed competitor study (GEM-CRM-002 → LENS 01b). Extra features are **backend modules**, switched on per client by business type. |
| D-05 | 2026-10-09 | **Menu approved** as proposed in `01b_LENS_COMPETITORS_MENU_MODULES.md` §7: Overview · CRM (Leads, Contacts, Deals + Pipeline, Tasks, Import) · Marketing (Ads, Ads insights, Segments, Forms, SEO) · module groups shown only when enabled (Commerce; later Restaurants) · Insights (Reports, Journeys & tracking) · Administration (Integrations, Users & roles, Settings, agency-only Modules & presets). General Reports stay in Insights; ad analysis lives in Marketing > Ads insights; a shared "Revenue by campaign" report is reachable from both. Ad-account connections live only in Administration > Integrations; Marketing > Ads shows a status chip and a Connect empty state. |
| D-07 | 2026-10-09 | **Answers to SPARK 02 §7, Q1–Q8.** These are the Lead's proposals; Ahmed said "apply your suggestions". (1) The agency roll-up across clients shows aggregates only: no contact rows, no audience mixing. (2) "Qualified" is one rule per client, set by the manager, with the default coming from the business-type preset; reps and managers may mark it. (3) Live and static segments both exist; "Save filter as segment" stays separate from personal saved views. (4) Speed-to-lead SLA is a per-client setting, **default 15 minutes within working hours**. The Lead chose that number, and Ahmed can change it. (5) Spend, cost per lead and CRM-ROAS are visible to the client through a per-client agency toggle, default on for the client manager only. (6) A hand-entered won deal value counts, labelled "Won deal value", not "paid revenue". Each client has one main currency, default EGP. (7) SEO for now is an organic-landing view only. (8) Roll-up money is shown per client in its own currency, with no conversion. |
| D-08 | 2026-10-09 | **Theme base = HeroUI** (Ahmed chose it from the gallery `03c_THEME_GALLERY.html`; React v3, built on React Aria + Tailwind v4, MIT per GEM-CRM-003; the licence and RTL support still need verification). The Systronic identity (`TSST/brands/t-sag/assets/IDENTITY.md`) is applied on top. The theme v1 (`03a_THEME/`) is rejected as generic. |
| D-09 | 2026-10-09 | **Official Systronic UI palette (Ahmed: "these are The Systronic colours"):** Royal Blue `#00338D`, Impact Red `#C60C30`, Legacy Blue `#002244`, Crisp White `#F2F3F3`, **plus a Cyan** that matches their saturation and mood, so it never looks out of place next to them (CREATIVE derives it; Ahmed confirms). This is the **official Systronic palette**. Every other colour is a derived tint or shade. They must be distributed correctly across **charts, hover and active** states. Source image: an art-direction colour page from the New York Giants creative toolkit; we take the colours only, not their layout or fonts. Open point: the logo's measured red `#E7213A` and navy `#181D36` (IDENTITY.md) differ from this palette. |
| D-10 | 2026-10-09 | Digits: Western digits (0-9) in both AR and EN UI, as on the delivery pages. This is the Lead's default; Ahmed did not object. |
| D-11 | 2026-10-09 | Ahmed approves HeroUI officially, plus the theme and the contact-record wireframe. **Sidebar brand block:** the iconic mark (vector) beside the word "The Systronic" set in the official font **Raleway** (wordmark style, as in the logo), both given a proper size. Under the brand it shows: the app version, a connection status (online/offline or connected), and the workspace manager. Ahmed wants this block to be a highlighted, important area. The block surface `#001A35` is the one deliberate exception to the "nothing darker than Legacy Blue" dark rule. |
