## B. Colour harmony rules (from mood — colour only)
- Base is dark petrol; warm colours arrive as one block plus warm light, never as a second base.
- Warm = terracotta/orange + stone/cream; cool = petrol. Ratio warm:cool about 40:60.
- One saturated accent per composition (orange OR terracotta block, not both large).
- Light is warm, low, from the side (golden hour, ~2700-3200K cove light); shadows stay soft and warm-dark, not black.
- Neutrals (cream/stone) carry the middle; marble veining is the "texture", not patterns.
- Photos #7-#9 already match this (cove light, stone, brass lamps): let them lead, palette frames them.
DO NOT copy from mood: arches, big quarter-circle shapes, monitor/phone mock-ups, flat-lay boards, kitchen render scenes, poster layout, serif headline style, "Kitchens Made For Real Life" text, the mood's logo lockup.

## G. VILL Visual Style v1 (grounded in reference/social-direction; principles, not layouts)
Read of Lugar (6 files): every frame is a SEAMLESS colour field (wall = floor, no horizon), ONE hero piece, one hard directional light that draws a shaft or leaf-gobo shadow across the field, low-mid camera, big empty colour around a small-ish subject. Kitchens/Screenshot files: colour harmony only; their text is ignored.
1. Set: one seamless field per collection, a brand colour with a darker falloff to the corners (Lugar red/blue/yellow/beige). Floor and wall in the same hue; the shadow, not an edge, builds the space.
2. Light: single warm key from a high side window, ~30-45 deg, HARD edge (sun-like), shadows warm-dark never black. Light lands ON the made detail (grain, brass, edge, stone relief); it is brightest there, the field falls off around it.
3. Shafts and gobos: a diagonal shaft crosses floor and wall into the subject (yellow tables, orange console); a leaf/foliage gobo adds life on beige sets. Use sparingly: one shaft per frame, never stacked.
4. Camera: lens eye height of the piece (waist/table height for furniture), slight 3/4 angle, 50-85 mm look, no wide distortion. Top-down 45 deg only for tables/surfaces. Vertical lines stay vertical.
5. Placement: subject sits on the lower-third line, off-centre by 10-20%, with 40-55% of the frame as calm negative space on the side the light comes from. Never centred and symmetrical unless it is a front elevation.
6. One-subject rule: ONE hero per frame. Pairs only when they are one set (two tables). Details come on the NEXT slide, never in the same frame; no props that compete.
7. Colour collections (one hue per post/series) mapped to approved palette: Petrol collection = petrol #01304A field, deep petrol #011C2C falloff, orange accent piece/light; Terracotta = #A04418 field; Cream/Stone = #EFE6D6 / #B9A48B field with leaf shadow; Orange = accent only (one small element, <=8%). White #F4F4F4 is for light/logo, not a field. Supporting colours are never mixed on one field.
8. Carousel grammar: slide 1 = context hero (place, sea, whole set) with the quietest logo; slides 2..n = one detail each, light lands on it, camera closer each step; vary crop and subject position slide to slide; last slide may return wide. No text unless LENS supplies one short line (Lugar-like: often none).
9. Logo: Wordmark Dark Transparent (white+orange) ~15% of width, top-left, margin 72 px on 1080 wide, on the quietest, darkest patch (sky, ceiling). Never over detail or bright wall; if no quiet area exists, leave it off that slide rather than box it.
10. Tone: only a subtle deep-petrol falloff to pull the eye to the lit detail. Never fake light, never glow.
HONEST GAP, real photos vs Lugar studio/CGI sets:
- CAN: warm cove light and backlit stone already give "light leads the eye"; real materials (stone relief, wood, marble, woven lamps) carry authenticity Lugar's CGI cannot; carousel hero-then-detail works; framing and falloff are controllable by crop.
- CANNOT: no seamless colour field (rooms have walls, clutter, vents, sockets); no single hard shaft or gobo; no one-subject isolation (several elements per frame); no colour-collection control (rooms are beige/brown/white); crop-only detail is capped near 1.25x upscale of a 1086x1448 retouched phone image, so true macro details are impossible; light direction cannot be re-aimed by us.
- Consequence: v1 here is "light leads the eye" by crop + tone only. Reaching Lugar's floor needs either (a) ChatGPT relight edits (RETOUCH_REQUESTS.md) or (b) a shoot or CGI of VILL furniture/kitchens on seamless petrol/terracotta/cream sets. Real-interior carousels are context/proof posts; product-collection posts need (b).
Post-01 logo rule: logo on slide 1 only (the cover carries the brand; detail slides stay clean and Lugar-like, and no quiet dark patch exists on slides 2-3 for a white mark).
