## Shared inputs, uncertainty and hypotheses

Inputs: original brief; confirmed operating profile and business context; governance, own agent contract and relevant registry; existing ANVIL boards and marketing-plan reference. Current request wins over conflicting historical scope instructions. User-supplied context is not independently audited.

Missing inputs: launch date, verified handles/service area, actual reach baselines, account-specific objective/minimum availability, editable logo/font files and rights, production capacity/footage. Each specialist labels assumptions, limits dependent promises and specifies a preflight validation owner. Unknowns do not prevent local planning.

Freshness: official platform mechanics checked for this pilot; source date/retrieval date and access limitations recorded. Never use fictional evaluation fixtures as ANVIL evidence.

Hypothesis and recommendation: repeated explicit name mapping improves transition comprehension; strategy and budget are recommendations, not observed outcomes. Metrics and decision rules live in 07_MEASUREMENT.

Reviews: each specialist self-checks and records another specialist's strength, risk and actionable improvement. Returned defects and rationale must survive integration. Next authorized action is local drafting/revision only. Internal acceptance does not imply user approval, execution authority or readiness promotion.
