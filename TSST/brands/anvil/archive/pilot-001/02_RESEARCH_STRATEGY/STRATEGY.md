## Executive recommendation

**اسم جديد. امتداد لنفس الحكاية.** The switch is a new chapter of an established business. Put the explicit relationship **Shark X Store → ANVIL SUPPS** ahead of abstract performance language from reveal onward. The premium identity signals ambition; it is not evidence that operations, authenticity controls, service speed, staff, ownership or product outcomes have improved.

Two days of anchored anticipation, one decisive reveal, then four distinct reasons to remember the name: explain continuity; show a real person introducing the next chapter; answer the transition question; consolidate the name. Teasers identify Shark and describe a next chapter, so suspense does not depend on suggesting closure. Limit paid teasing in this small pilot: a comprehensible reveal has a stronger connection to the objective than an unbranded mystery impression. SPARK owns exact allocation and feasibility.

## Audience logic — jobs, not invented personas

Method: lightweight contextual JTBD hypotheses for communication, not a validated ICP or market segmentation study. No invented demographics, income, gym membership, pains, customer quotations, gender split or segment sizes.

| People who know Shark informally, ASSUMPTION | On encountering ANVIL, connect prior recognition to this name | Recognize the relationship without following the whole sequence | Prior recognition not measured | D3/D4 must stand alone; do not require seeing teaser |
| New adult fitness/supplement-category viewers, candidate audience | When seeing an unfamiliar name, understand the category and decide whether to follow | Remember ANVIL SUPPS; understand it as the supplements brand in the brief | Product demand, eligibility, service geography unknown | Lead with ANVIL, brief category context, secondary transition label |

Buyer, supplement user and follower may be different people; this pilot measures communication response, not buyer qualification. Do not infer need for supplements from appearance. Paid geographic/age eligibility belongs to SPARK's verified preflight; EGP does not prove shipping coverage. Exclude purchase pressure, minors as a creative target, health-result promises, physique comparisons and unsubstantiated product authenticity demonstrations.

Alternative audience behaviors are hypotheses: ignore a cryptic teaser, assume ANVIL is unrelated, continue searching the old name, or follow the new identity. No named competitors or superiority claim is needed. Differentiation in this campaign is **recognizable continuity expressed through the supplied identity**, not a validated market-exclusive business capability.

## Message house and claim boundaries

1. Relationship: **Shark X Store بيتطوّر لـ ANVIL SUPPS.** Supported by E01.
2. Continuity: **اسم جديد. امتداد لنفس الحكاية.** Communicates the brief's continuity without claiming unchanged legal entity, people, terms or systems.
3. Future: **مرحلة جديدة بهوية أقوى.** Aspirational identity direction; avoid turning it into a verified operational improvement.
4. Behavior: **اتعرّف على ANVIL SUPPS** at reveal, **تابع ANVIL SUPPS للمرحلة الجاية** during reinforcement. Use follow action on verified official surface; no guessed handle.

Reserve “Forged Performance” as optional brand signature on D7 only; it cannot displace the name mapping. Omit “Authentic products”, “Trusted brands” and “Real Brands. Real Progress.” as unsupported product-level proof in this pilot. The source lists them as verbal territory, which does not establish authenticity testing or authorization. Prelaunch snapshot: record available previous seven-day native metrics and follower count only if access is explicitly approved later. If unavailable, mark baseline unavailable. Zero is not a missing-data code. Daily Lead triage focuses on confusion and factual questions; SPARK evaluates spend/delivery; CREATIVE corrects legibility or crops. D7 closeout is provisional; fix an agreed export cutoff and allow platform reporting lag before final report.

Success is understandable transition at usable delivery cost, not a sales total. Promising signals: multiple independent voluntary replies correctly link both names, profile activity accompanies adequate exposure, and completed views are not dominated by confusion. Report actual counts and denominators. No invented success percentage or significance claim. For one serious factual misconception, correct the relevant text; for repeated confusion from distinct respondents, prioritize D4/D6 explanation over optional polish. This is a conservative editorial decision rule, not a statistical threshold.

Do not conclude long-term brand lift, market penetration, incremental sales, lifetime value, representative audience preference, platform superiority or validated media benchmarks from seven days and 1,500 EGP. Carry forward verified copy, reusable visual masters, coded questions and hypotheses; do not automatically scale spend.
