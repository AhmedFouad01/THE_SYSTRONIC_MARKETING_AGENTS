# ANVIL SUPPS — The Switch Campaign

TSST-PILOT-001 · خطة للمراجعة والاعتماد · 2026-09-22

## The decision / الفكرة الأساسية

Make the new name familiar without discarding the old name's history. The campaign's essential communication is **Shark X Store → ANVIL SUPPS**: an established story enters a stronger visual and professional chapter. The stronger claim concerns the identity and direction; it is not a claim that a supplement improves strength or health.

The old name is the recognition bridge. ANVIL is the destination. From reveal day onward, every standalone core, TikTok edit and Story set explains that relationship, so viewers do not need to have seen previous episodes. Do not rely on an abstract logo reveal to transfer recognition.

## Audience logic

Existing Shark followers/customers need reassurance that ANVIL is the evolution being announced. People who know Shark but do not closely follow its pages need an explicit name connection. New category-adjacent adult viewers need to remember ANVIL and understand what is being introduced without knowing Shark's history. These are communication needs, not verified audience sizes or separate mandatory ad sets.

Use existing channels organically to reach people already familiar with the business. The proposed paid audience is one eligible adult geography-based audience after confirming the actual service area. Avoid subdividing the small budget by personas, platform, age bands and retargeting pools. No customer-list upload or account access is part of this package.

## Seven-day structure

| Day | Phase | Core narrative job | Core format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TEASE | Something is evolving within the familiar Shark story | Anticipation video |
| 2 | TEASE | Reassure that this is a next chapter; next reveal is approaching (use tomorrow only after dates are locked) | Continuity static |
| 3 | REVEAL | State Shark X Store → ANVIL SUPPS unmistakably | Reveal film |
| 4 | REINFORCE | Explain what the switch means and distinguish known facts from promises | Continuity carousel |
| 5 | REINFORCE | Give the new identity a human/identity context | Real-person/identity film with approved fallback |
| 6 | REINFORCE | Answer transition questions without inventing operational assurances | FAQ carousel |
| 7 | REINFORCE | Repeat the new name and invite following the next chapter | Name-memory film |

Each day also has a TikTok-native video treatment and a three-frame Facebook/Instagram Story set. Core inventory: four video concepts, one static, two carousels; plus seven TikTok edits and seven Story sets = **21 editorial deliverables**. The Story sets contain 21 frames. Platform exports, aspect-ratio adapters and paid cutdowns are not extra editorial concepts; see production manifest for exact file inventory.

## Paid investment recommendation

**1,500 EGP total media ceiling: Meta 1,500; TikTok paid 0.** TikTok remains an organic channel. SPARK's dated official-source research explains why the suggested 400 EGP TikTok Ads Manager allocation is not a workable default and flags category eligibility requirements. This change concentrates a small awareness pilot rather than creating a second underfunded buying structure.

## Creative direction

Industrial premium, off-black and black fields, white typography, restrained ANVIL orange, sharp angles, strong negative space and cinematic real-person details. Cairo carries Arabic; Monument Extended carries major English headlines; Inter carries English supporting text. Original supplied ANVIL logo geometry remains intact. Use deliberate cuts and linear masks around the mark, not logo morphing, decorative fire, saturation overload or generic supplement promises.

Copy prioritizes the name bridge, the next-chapter message, then a light awareness CTA. “Meet ANVIL SUPPS” and following the next chapter are appropriate; discounts and shop-now pressure are not the organizing behavior. Final exact copy, safe composition, timings, shots and platform edits are specified by CREATIVE.

## Learning and next decision

Evaluate whether people understand the relationship and remember the ANVIL name. Track platform-scoped reach and frequency, duration-specific video completion, meaningful engagement, profile/page activity where available, and manually coded transition responses. Report counts and denominators; missing data is missing, not zero. Cross-platform reach is not deduplicated and paid/organic activity is not automatically attributable to the campaign.

Promising evidence combines usable delivery with independent responses recognizing the switch and ANVIL. Persistent “did Shark close?” questions trigger clearer continuity explanation. Weak viewing retention triggers an opening/copy edit; weak name comprehension triggers a clearer name bridge, not an unsupported audience conclusion. Sparse observations call for another bounded learning period, not a declared winner.

One week and 1,500 EGP cannot establish incremental brand lift, long-term CPM/CPA benchmarks, sales causality or a durable platform winner. Carry forward assets that pass final readability QA, recurring customer language, unresolved questions and the complete measurement definitions; keep observations separate from hypotheses. Detailed thresholds and decision rules are in 07_MEASUREMENT.
