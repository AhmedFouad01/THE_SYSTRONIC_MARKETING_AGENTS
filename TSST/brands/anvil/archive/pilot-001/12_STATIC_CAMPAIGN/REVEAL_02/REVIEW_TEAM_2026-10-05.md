## Findings (criteria: concept, copy, hierarchy, type, layout, imagery, distinctiveness, CTA)
1. **Both names appear twice (user note confirmed, HIGH within asset).** "Shark X Store" and "ANVIL SUPPS" are on the book pages AND again as the three-line bridge in the footer. The page names are small and low-contrast "caption" text on empty black pages; the footer repeats them larger. Result: the book is just a prop for text already stated, and the viewer reads the same pair twice. In Reveal 01 the bag carried the transformation *and* the top bridge too (same duplication, accepted earlier) — do not carry that flaw forward; it also costs hierarchy.
2. **Concept (SK-012): clichéd metaphor, weak transformation.** "New chapter" book is the most predictable rebrand metaphor; unlike the bag-with-diagonal, nothing changes *across* the object: left page is plain text, right page has the logo. The orange bookmark is a thin vertical line that doesn't mark a chapter turn; it runs out below the book into dead dark space (~150 px) and points at the footer. No orange diagonal link to Reveal 01's grammar beyond colour.
3. **Reading direction (Egyptian audience).** Latin-style book: old name on the left page, new on the right. In an Arabic reading context the sequence reads right-to-left, so "old to new" runs backwards against the Arabic headline above. Flag: either mirror the book logic (old on right, new on left) or accept and justify; currently unconsidered in QA.
4. **Hierarchy/typography.** Arabic headline "نفس الحكاية. فصل جديد." is clear (best element) but it is a statement, not a hook; footer bridge uses Inter for English and a small Cairo "بقى اسمه" with weak weight ("بقى اسمه" is the smallest and most important connector). Pages have large empty black areas: left page almost blank with a small label, right page logo + small caption. Minimalism rule says empty must be authored: here it reads as unused paper.
5. **Imagery.** Generated charcoal book is credible (clean spine, shadow, page edges); dark-on-dark gives low depth; the overall frame is very dark and uniform — thumbnails at feed scale look like a black rectangle with white text. Provenance: generated, labelled conceptual; fine.
6. **Copy/LENS.** Message hierarchy is right (same story to new name; no unsupported claims; no CTA is correct for reassurance). Risk: "نفس الحكاية" + "فصل جديد" promises continuity of the Shark X Store story — within the user-confirmed name-change fact only. Keep: no same-team/same-product claims.
7. **Distinctiveness.** Remove the logo: still a black stock book mock-up — not specifically ANVIL. Reveal 01 at least had the orange diagonal cut. Series cohesion is thin.

## Direction for v02
- Keep headline and the "no CTA, no claims" gate. Keep the book only if it *transforms*: e.g., left page = Shark X Store set in its own recognizable treatment, a single orange diagonal/tear/fold crossing the spread (reuse Reveal 01 diagonal) so the old page literally turns into the new one.
- Say each name **once**: choose either names-on-pages (large, designed, with the "بقى اسمه" connector between them on the spine) or footer bridge; drop the other. Put the connector where the eye travels.
- Make the old name a designed lockup (reference old identity if available; ask the user), not default Inter text.
- Resolve RTL page order; kill the dangling ribbon or make it the chapter marker that ends on the new page.
- Lift tonal range: a warmer edge light or a mid-grey ground so the thumbnail has depth.
- Alternative if book stays weak: continue the Reveal 01 carrier family (same object, different view/angle) for stronger series grammar.
