# TSST-LEAD-SK-007 — Positioning and Product Marketing

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-007`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for product evidence, alternatives and target buying context. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Ask LENS to substantiate audience and alternatives; select a defensible value proposition, proof hierarchy and objections; connect product capabilities to buyer outcomes and sales enablement.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver positioning decision and product message brief with claim-evidence map. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

LENS proposes positioning; Lead integrates it within approved strategy. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
