# TSST-LEAD-SK-005 — Growth Experimentation and Learning Portfolio

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-005`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for growth hypotheses and scarce testing resources. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Rank hypotheses by decision impact, uncertainty and affordable evidence; select the smallest credible test, predeclare success and stop rules; include failed hypotheses in the learning log.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver ranked learning backlog with owners, cost caps and decisions unlocked. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Growth hacking does not authorize deceptive tactics or uncontrolled production experiments. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
