# TSST-LEAD-SK-023 — Prioritization and Portfolio Tradeoffs

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-023`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for initiative backlog, objective and resource constraints. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Compare expected impact, confidence, effort, reversibility and dependencies; expose double-counted benefits; retain mandatory work and explicitly defer lower-value work.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver ranked portfolio with rationale, rejected alternatives and review triggers. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

A numerical ranking is decision support, not false precision. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
