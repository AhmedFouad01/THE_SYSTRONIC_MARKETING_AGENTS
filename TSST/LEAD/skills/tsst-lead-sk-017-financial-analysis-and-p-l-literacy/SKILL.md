# TSST-LEAD-SK-017 — Financial Analysis and P&L Literacy

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-017`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for dated financial statements, accounting basis and currency. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Reconcile revenue, direct costs, gross profit and operating expenses; distinguish profit, cash and working capital; inspect mix and period comparability; identify missing ledger evidence.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver P&L bridge, cash implications and decision-sensitive assumptions. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Do not supply tax or accounting sign-off or infer unavailable financials. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
