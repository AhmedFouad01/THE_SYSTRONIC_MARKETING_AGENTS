# TSST-LEAD-SK-011 — Customer Research and Consumer Psychology

Owner: TSST Lead

## Inputs and trigger

Use for customer decisions, interviews and observed behavior. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Commission LENS to investigate triggers, jobs, barriers and purchase context; distinguish reported preferences from observed action; consider sampling and confirmation bias; translate insights into testable offer implications.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver customer decision model with quotes or records, uncertainties and research gaps. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Never invent personas or exploit sensitive vulnerabilities. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
