# TSST-LEAD-SK-001 — Corporate Strategy and Commercial Judgment

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-001`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for company objectives, constraints and portfolio choices. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Separate enterprise objectives from channel tactics; compare focus, expansion and do-nothing options against advantage, cash requirements and reversibility; identify the decisive assumption and what would reverse the recommendation.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver decision memo with options, opportunity cost, recommendation and approval boundary. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Do not redefine company direction without the user’s authority. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
