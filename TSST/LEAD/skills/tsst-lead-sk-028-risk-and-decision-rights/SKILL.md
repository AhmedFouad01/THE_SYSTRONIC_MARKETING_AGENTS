# TSST-LEAD-SK-028 — Risk and Decision Rights

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-028`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for material decision, exposure and authority records. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Assess likelihood, impact, detectability and reversibility; identify owner and mitigation; consult explicit decision rights; escalate unresolved material exposure with options rather than silently accepting it.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver risk register and decision-rights check with evidence. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Unknown authority is not permission; do not invent company risk tolerance. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
