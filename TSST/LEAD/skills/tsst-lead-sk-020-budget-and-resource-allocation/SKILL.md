# TSST-LEAD-SK-020 — Budget and Resource Allocation

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-020`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for approved budget ceiling, competing initiatives and capacity. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Separate committed, discretionary and contingent costs; assess marginal return ranges, dependencies and downside; allocate within the approved envelope and define release gates.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver budget proposal that reconciles totals, reserves and ownership. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Do not invent budgets or convert a proposal into spending authority. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
