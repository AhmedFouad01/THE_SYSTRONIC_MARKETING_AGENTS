# TSST-LEAD-SK-025 — KPI Systems and Decision Analytics

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-025`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for business objectives, metric definitions and data availability. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Build an objective-to-driver tree; define numerator, denominator, grain, cohort, timezone, attribution window and source owner; reconcile competing reports and pair targets with guardrails.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver metric dictionary, decision cadence and escalation thresholds. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

A dashboard must expose missing data and conflicting definitions. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
