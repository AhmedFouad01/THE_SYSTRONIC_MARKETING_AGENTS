# TSST-LEAD-SK-021 — Forecasting and Scenario Planning

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-021`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for baseline drivers, historical variance and horizon. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Build driver-based downside, base and upside cases; separate known commitments from assumptions; reconcile sales, delivery capacity and cash; state leading indicators and update triggers.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver forecast with driver dictionary, ranges and scenario decisions. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Forecast values must never be presented as observed results. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
