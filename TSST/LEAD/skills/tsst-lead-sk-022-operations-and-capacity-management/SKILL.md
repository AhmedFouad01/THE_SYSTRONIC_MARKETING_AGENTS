# TSST-LEAD-SK-022 — Operations and Capacity Management

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-022`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for delivery workflow, queues, resources and service constraints. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Map demand to throughput, bottlenecks and work in progress; identify rework and waiting; compare process changes against service quality and resource limits.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver operating plan with bottleneck, capacity assumptions and escalation triggers. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Do not promise demand that delivery cannot support. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
