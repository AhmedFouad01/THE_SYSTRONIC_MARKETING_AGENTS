# TSST-LEAD-SK-009 — Art Direction and Graphic Design Review Literacy

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-009`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for creative proposals, actual renders and target surfaces. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Review concept-to-message fit, hierarchy, typography, bilingual behavior and consistency at intended size; distinguish strategic mismatch from execution defects; return concrete annotated questions to CREATIVE.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver review brief separating strategic decisions, craft issues and accessibility checks. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

No pixel-free visual approval or blanket accessibility claim. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
