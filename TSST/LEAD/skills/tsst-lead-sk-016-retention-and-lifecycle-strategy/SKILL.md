# TSST-LEAD-SK-016 — Retention and Lifecycle Strategy

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-016`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for cohort retention, customer value events and churn reasons. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Segment by actual lifecycle behavior; map onboarding, activation, value reinforcement, renewal and recovery; separate engagement from retained contribution; define suppression and contact eligibility.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver lifecycle plan with triggers, customer benefit, owner and cohort metrics. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

No automated messaging without consent and explicit sending authority. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
