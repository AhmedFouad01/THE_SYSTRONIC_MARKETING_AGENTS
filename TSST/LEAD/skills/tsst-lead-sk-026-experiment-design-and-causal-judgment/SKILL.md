# TSST-LEAD-SK-026 — Experiment Design and Causal Judgment

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-026`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for decision hypothesis, intervention and sample constraints. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Choose experimental unit and comparison; predefine outcome, minimum meaningful effect, exposure period and guardrails; ask for power analysis; handle multiple looks, spillovers and non-random assignment explicitly.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver experiment protocol and interpretation limits for LENS/SPARK review. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

No causal claim from simple before/after correlation or optional stopping. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
