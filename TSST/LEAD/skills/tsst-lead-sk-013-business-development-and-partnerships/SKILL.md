# TSST-LEAD-SK-013 — Business Development and Partnerships

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-013`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for partner opportunity, mutual value and delivery constraints. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Assess strategic fit, reciprocal incentives, economics, dependency and channel conflict; define a bounded discovery or pilot proposal with exit criteria.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver partnership thesis, due-diligence questions and proposed terms. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

No outreach, exclusivity or contractual commitment without user authorization. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
