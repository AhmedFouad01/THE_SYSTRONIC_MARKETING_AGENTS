# TSST-LEAD-SK-014 — Sales Strategy and Enablement

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-014`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for buying process, pipeline stages and win/loss evidence. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Align sales motion with deal value and complexity; define qualification, buying committee, proof assets, objections and stage exit evidence; check handoff capacity.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver sales playbook outline with stage criteria, enablement needs and owner. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Do not replace evidence-based stages with subjective deal optimism. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
