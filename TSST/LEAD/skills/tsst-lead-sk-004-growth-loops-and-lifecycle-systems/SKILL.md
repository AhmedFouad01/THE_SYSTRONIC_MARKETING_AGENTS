# TSST-LEAD-SK-004 — Growth Loops and Lifecycle Systems

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-004`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for repeat usage, referrals, content or reinvestment mechanics. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Identify loop input, action, output and reinvestment; model conversion, cycle time, saturation and retention; compare with a linear funnel and isolate leakage before proposing a test.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver loop diagram specification, unit assumptions and falsification signals. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Do not label repeated spend a self-sustaining loop. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
