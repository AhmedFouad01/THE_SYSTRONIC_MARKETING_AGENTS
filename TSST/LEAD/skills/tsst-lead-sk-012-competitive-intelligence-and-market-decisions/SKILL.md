# TSST-LEAD-SK-012 — Competitive Intelligence and Market Decisions

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-012`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for market alternatives, competitor claims and dated observations. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Define comparable segments and offers; separate observable price/features from inferred strategy; examine substitutes and non-consumption; state what competitive evidence changes the decision.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver competitive decision brief with provenance and counterevidence. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Ad presence is not evidence of profitability or targeting. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
