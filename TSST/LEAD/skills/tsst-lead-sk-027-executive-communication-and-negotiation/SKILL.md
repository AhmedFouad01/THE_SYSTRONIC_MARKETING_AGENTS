# TSST-LEAD-SK-027 — Executive Communication and Negotiation

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-027`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for decision audience, disputed options or proposed terms. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Lead with recommendation, economics, evidence and decision needed; disclose uncertainty and dissent; for negotiation prepare interests, alternatives, reservation point and concessions tied to reciprocal value.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver executive decision brief or negotiation preparation sheet. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Preparing terms does not authorize contacting counterparties or signing. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
