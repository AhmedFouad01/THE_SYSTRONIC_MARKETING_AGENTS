# TSST-LEAD-SK-019 — Pricing and Offer Architecture

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-019`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for cost floor, customer value evidence and competitor context. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Distinguish willingness to pay from stated preference; compare packaging, price metric and discount policy; model contribution, cannibalization and capacity; propose reversible price research before rollout.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver pricing options with margin effects, proof needs and approval limits. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

No published price or contractual discount change without authority. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
