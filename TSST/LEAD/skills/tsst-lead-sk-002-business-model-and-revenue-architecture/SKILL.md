# TSST-LEAD-SK-002 — Business Model and Revenue Architecture

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-002`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for products, services, revenue streams and delivery costs. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Map who pays, for what, how often and through which channel; connect customer value to delivery activities, partners and cost drivers; distinguish cash timing from earned revenue.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver business model with revenue mechanics, dependencies and evidence gaps. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

A canvas is a hypothesis map, not validated demand. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
