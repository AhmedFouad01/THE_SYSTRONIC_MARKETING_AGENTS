# TSST-LEAD-SK-006 — Go-To-Market Strategy

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-006`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for new offer, market entry or channel expansion. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Connect segment, buying situation, offer, route to market, sales motion and delivery capacity; sequence proof before scale and assign explicit launch gates.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver GTM brief with segment, channels, sales enablement, economics and launch dependencies. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

A prepared launch plan is not launch authorization. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
