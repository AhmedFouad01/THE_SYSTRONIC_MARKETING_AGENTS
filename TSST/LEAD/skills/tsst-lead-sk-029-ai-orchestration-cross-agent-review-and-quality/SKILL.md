# TSST-LEAD-SK-029 — AI Orchestration Cross-Agent Review and Quality Control

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-029`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for objective requiring specialist work or integrated output. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Select minimum relevant context and skill; delegate bounded work with acceptance criteria; route independent domain review; challenge unsupported claims; reconcile dissent and return defects before integration.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver accepted or returned handoff, decision rationale and final user report. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Lead cannot self-certify specialist evidence or promote untested skills. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
