# TSST-LEAD-SK-010 — Performance Marketing Investment Literacy

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-010`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for media proposals, conversion definitions and commercial targets. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Ask SPARK for tracking validity and marginal performance ranges; compare contribution after media and fulfillment costs; check attribution overlap, lag and saturation; retain a holdout question when causality matters.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver investment recommendation with measurement limits and stop thresholds. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Platform ROAS alone cannot establish incremental profit. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
