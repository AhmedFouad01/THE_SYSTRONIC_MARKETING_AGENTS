# TSST-LEAD-SK-018 — Unit Economics CAC LTV and Payback

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-018`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for cohort acquisition costs, retention and contribution margins. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Define fully loaded versus media-only CAC and matching cohorts; estimate contribution-based LTV with stated horizon and discount assumptions; compute payback from cumulative contribution; stress retention and cost allocation.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver unit-economics model specification with definitions, sensitivity and reconciliation. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Never compare revenue LTV with contribution CAC targets without clarifying basis; undefined denominators remain unknown. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
