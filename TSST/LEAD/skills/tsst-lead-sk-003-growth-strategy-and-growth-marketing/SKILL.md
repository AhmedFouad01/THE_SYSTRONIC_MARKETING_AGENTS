# TSST-LEAD-SK-003 — Growth Strategy and Growth Marketing

Owner: TSST Lead  
Version: 1.0  
Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Evaluation: not run; future reservation `TSST-LEAD-EV-003`.

## Inputs and trigger

Use for growth target, baseline and funnel or cohort data. Read the confirmed operating profile and the user's current objective. Identify missing decision-critical inputs before recommending action; unknowns remain explicit.

## Method

Decompose growth into acquisition, activation, retention, expansion and price; locate the binding constraint; compare interventions by incremental contribution and capacity before recommending a portfolio.

## Output and acceptance

Deliver growth thesis, bottleneck evidence, interventions and learning priorities. Show the decision supported, evidence versus assumptions, rejected alternatives, owner and next authorized step. A reviewer should be able to trace the recommendation back to inputs and identify what would change it.

## Boundary and failure check

Revenue growth without margin or retention analysis is insufficient. Apply the [Lead operating contract](../../AGENT.md). Check that the output resolves the triggering decision, preserves specialist ownership and does not claim validation from its own completion.
