Status: APPROVED WORKING METHOD. Owner: Ahmed Fouad. Date: 2026-10-12 (Africa/Cairo).
Scope: reusable across brands, including VILL Studio and ANVIL SUPPS.
Authority: owner explicitly requested this method be saved in main memory and TSST memory for reuse on ANVIL SUPPS. This is workflow approval, not asset approval, readiness promotion, or campaign launch authority.

## Roles and discussion
- Codex/TSST Lead handles understanding, creative direction, difficult decisions, concise handoffs, and final review. Reduce Codex context loading and execution overhead.
- Discuss and confirm material requirements before production or dispatch. Do not generate or delegate while the owner is still clarifying the brief.
- Ahmed manually passes the English handoff to Antigravity or Gemini and returns its report. No direct automated connection is assumed.
- Antigravity is the file-capable external execution surface (ROLE B), not a reinstated TSST runtime. Gemini app work needs uploaded/pasted context, not inaccessible local paths. Existing runtime/roster decisions remain intact.

## Two stages — one asset batch
1. PRODUCTION / REVIEW: external tool selects approved-scope source assets, generates the agreed count, saves outputs and an original-versus-final contact sheet, and returns one compact report. No Delivery upload or page integration at this stage. Lead reviews quality and product fidelity; Ahmed accepts or requests bounded changes.
2. DELIVERY: only after Ahmed explicitly approves the images and requests delivery, send a separate English packaging brief. Reuse the approved outputs, pair originals and finals, preserve previous versions, and integrate into the existing shared brand Delivery System. No generation in this stage unless separately authorized.

Two stages do not mean two image batches. Five images was the agreed table batch, not a universal batch size.

## Handoff and execution
- State ROLE: EXTERNAL TOOL, SURFACE, objective, exact source/read list, allowed output paths, count, dimensions, brand identity, creative concept, product invariants, budget/call cap, acceptance criteria, report format, and stop condition.
- Select strong distinct products from the existing bank where available; inspect actual originals and avoid duplicate designs. Source bank remains read-only.
- Give each product a distinct cinematic concept, camera angle and lighting plan while retaining a coherent campaign direction. Wide/fisheye/forced perspective are optional tools, not permission to lose product geometry or crop required pieces.
- Preserve actual product design and all components of a set. Preserve/composite approved logos, packaging and label text; never invent brand facts, supplement claims, certifications or product variants.
- Reuse completed outputs; check interrupted work before another generation. No automatic rerolls or exploratory variants. State the agreed call limit and actual usage. Cost is unavailable if not shown; no credit purchase does not prove zero cost. No promised Codex savings percentage without measurement.
- Keep one concise brief, one combined report and one combined review. Avoid repeated boot reads, broad inventories, polling and unnecessary documentation.

## Write scope and traceability
- Every request, report, owner correction and Lead decision must be saved as readable files in the job's existing work area. Example: work/external/round-01/{request.md,report.md,review.md} plus a manifest of source/output paths and prompts. Keep these files out of client-facing pages.
- Do not depend solely on hidden subagent chats or runtime-private memory. Mark missing/encrypted historical messages as incomplete; never reconstruct them as verbatim evidence.
- ROLE B normally writes only declared output paths. A delivery request may name an explicit owner-approved allowlist of existing packaging/index files and delivery scripts. This exception is limited to that delivery task; otherwise the Lead/responsible TSST agent applies the integration. No unrestricted repo editing, bus operations, commits, main-memory writes or publishing authority is granted.
- Delivery uses the current generator and checks, including brand hub navigation and original/final media links. Preserve archives. Internal workflow details belong in internal delivery/evidence files.

## Cross-brand reuse
