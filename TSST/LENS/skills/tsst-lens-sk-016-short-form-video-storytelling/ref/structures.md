# Hook types and story structures

Source note: ABCD checkpoints from [Google](https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/). Timings below are TSST working defaults, not platform rules. Gemini figures (Meta ~1.7 s scroll decision, TikTok >70% scroll within 3 s, 60-70% 3 s retention = 4-7x impressions): **not confirmed**, do not quote.

## Hook types
| Type | First frame shows | Fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before/After contrast | Starkest "before" or the "after" first | Interiors, renovation | Long "before" loses viewer |
| Reveal | Final result glimpsed, then rewound | Project showcase | Spoils payoff if too complete |
| Visual interrupt | Unexpected motion or anomaly | Organic, TikTok | Gimmick vs quiet-premium |
| Question/curiosity | Short text question | Educational | Generic wording |
| Tension | Stakes or problem in frame | Paid problem-solution | Negative tone off-brand |

Rule: the hook carries the promise visually; text supports, never carries alone.

## Structure A - Before & After (6 s / 15 s)
| Beat | 6 s | 15 s | Purpose | Viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | 0-2 | 0-3 | Show the state to be changed | Empty shell / raw state |
| 2 Change | 2-4 | 3-10 | Catalyst, progress | Light moving, stages, materials |
| 3 Reveal | 4-6 | 10-13 | Payoff | Finished space |
| 4 Brand/CTA | (in 3) | 13-15 | Close | Logo or end card |
- Use: highly visual services. Mistake: too much "before"; cap at ~20% of runtime.

## Structure B - Problem & Solution (15 s / 30 s)
| Beat | 15 s | 30 s | Purpose | Viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | 0-3 | Pain named | Problem in frame |
| Empathy | 3-6 | 3-10 | Why it hurts | Familiar friction |
| Solution | 6-12 | 10-22 | Offer as relief | Service/product at work |
| Proof + CTA | 12-15 | 22-30 | Evidence, next step | Result, end card |
- Use: paid, specific pain. Mistake: feature-first opening. Proof must be real (SK-005).

## Structure C - Process / BTS (30 s / 60 s)
| Beat | 30 s | 60 s | Purpose | Viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | 0-3 | Glimpse of result | Final detail |
| Setup | 3-8 | 3-15 | Materials, plan | Raw materials, drawings |
| Craft | 8-24 | 15-45 | Trust via care | Satisfying detail steps |
| Reveal + CTA | 24-30 | 45-60 | Payoff | Finished piece, end card |
- Use: craftsmanship, trust. Mistake: repetitive shots held too long; change image every 2-4 s.
- With AI-regenerated shots keep process claims honest (do not imply footage of real work).

## Structure D - Day-in-the-life / narrative (60 s)
| Beat | Sec | Purpose | Viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | Enter the space | Door/window light |
| Context | 3-15 | Establish place | Wide, layout |
| Journey | 15-45 | Live in the space | Human scale, routine |
| Resolution | 45-60 | Emotional payoff | Calm, view, close |
- Use: real estate, lifestyle. Mistake: no arc or payoff; a tour is not a story.
