# Text, sound, brand moment

Platform specifics marked "not confirmed" have no official URL in this package. Check current Meta/TikTok/YouTube help pages before relying on them.

## On-screen text
| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Few words | TSST default 2-5 words per card (quiet-premium). TikTok's help page suggests denser text for performance ads ([source](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices)); not our tone, not adopted |
| One idea per card | Text adds meaning, never repeats the picture |
| Timing | Readable twice at natural pace; at least ~1.5 s (TSST default) |
| Safe zones | Keep text and logo off platform UI. Reported Reels zones (top ~14%, bottom ~35%): third-party, **not confirmed** |
| Contrast | Legible on every frame behind it |

## Sound-off first
- Story must work muted: picture plus text carry the whole message.
- Share of Meta viewers with sound: **not confirmed** (conflicting third-party figures).
- YouTube: Google states video is largely watched with sound on, so use audio for branding ([ABCDs](https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/)).
- Test: watch muted before sign-off.

## VO vs music
| Option | Use when | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Music only | Visual montage, quiet-premium reels | Cut on musical beats; soft, no drops |
| VO + captions | Explain, proof, process | Captions mandatory; VO and caption language match |
| Ambient/natural | Interiors, sea, materials | Premium feel; licence-safe |
Claim "VO + captions raises retention": not confirmed.

## Arabic / English rules
| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never mix AR and EN in one line | Bidi reordering breaks order and punctuation |
| Separate AR and EN versions (or separate cards) | Clean RTL/LTR |
| Arabic: right aligned, dialect (Egyptian/MSA) set in brief, large legible font | Legibility |
| Numbers/brand names inside an Arabic line: avoid, or give own card | Bidi |
| Lines of 2-5 words | Fewer wrap and bidi issues |

## Brand moment
| Placement | Rule |
|---|---|
| Early | Subtle presence in first ~3 s (material, object, small mark) without halting the story; ABCD Branding: integrate early and often |
| End | Static end card, logo plus one line, short hold |
| Logo | Small, inside safe zone, never over the reveal |
| Claims | Qualified and provable (SK-005) |

## CTA
| Context | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Organic | Soft: "Save", "Message us"; engagement-led |
| Paid | Direct, one action, e.g. "Get a quote"; wording/placement with SPARK |
| Quiet-premium | One calm line in end card; no urgency stunts |
