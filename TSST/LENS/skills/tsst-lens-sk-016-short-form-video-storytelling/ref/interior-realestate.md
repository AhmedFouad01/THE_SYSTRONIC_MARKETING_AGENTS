# Interior / fit-out / real-estate project stories

## Story levers
| Lever | How to use | Beat sheet note |
|---|---|---|
| Transformation | Empty shell -> finished; the spine of the story | Same camera position across stages helps continuity |
| Light as narrator | Sun path, shadows crossing floor/wall, golden hour, sea glow | State light direction and time per beat |
| Material close-ups | Wood grain, stone veining, fabric, metal edge | 1-2 s macro, one per beat, contrast with wide |
| Human scale | A hand, a figure at the window, a cup on the table | No face needed; suggests living |
| Reveal timing | Payoff in last third; hook shows the contrast | Do not show the finished space in full before the reveal |

## Quiet-premium rules
- Slow, steady camera (push-in, gentle pan); few cuts; no whip transitions.
- 2-5 words per text card; no price shouting, no emoji.
- Calm ambient or soft instrumental sound.
- Brand mark small, late.

## Source assets (owner rule)
Client phone photos/video are reference for layout, view and materials only. Each final shot is regenerated as a pro AI shot. The beat sheet maps reference to beat and lists what must stay true (layout, window position, view).
Honesty check: AI shots must not misrepresent the delivered unit (view, size, finish); flag any beat where the generated view goes beyond the real one.
