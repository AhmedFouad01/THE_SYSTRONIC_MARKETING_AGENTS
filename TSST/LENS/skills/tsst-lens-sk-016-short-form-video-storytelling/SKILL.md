# TSST-LENS-SK-016 Short-Form Video Storytelling

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective
Produce a story and beat sheet for a 6/15/30/60 s vertical video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts; ad or organic) before any shot is designed. LENS owns the story; CREATIVE owns the shot list and visuals; SPARK owns formats, KPIs and spend.

## When to use
Reel/short/video-ad briefs, project-showcase video, before/after, process, day-in-the-life. Not for editing craft or AI generation settings (CREATIVE).

## Owner rules (2026-10-08)
- Story first, then shots. No shot list without an approved beat sheet.
- Raw client phone photos/videos are reference only; every final shot is regenerated as a pro AI shot.
- Quiet-premium tone, few words on screen.
- Arabic and English on-screen text: never both in one line; short lines.

## Required method
1. Brief: audience, one promise, platform, length, organic vs paid, brand, available proof (claims per SK-005, plan fit per SK-006).
2. Hook: pick one type from `ref/structures.md`; the first frame already carries the promise visually.
3. Structure: choose by length and goal (`ref/structures.md`); one idea per video.
4. Beat sheet: table with beat | seconds | purpose | what viewer sees | text | sound | reference asset.
5. Text/sound plan: `ref/text-sound-brand.md`; design for sound-off first.
6. Brand moment: subtle early, end card with CTA; claims qualified.
7. Hand-off to CREATIVE: beat sheet plus the contract below. Interiors/real estate: `ref/interior-realestate.md`.

## Hand-off contract (each beat must state)
Seconds; story purpose; subject and what changes in frame; light/time of day; camera intent (static/push/pan); material or detail in focus; on-screen text (one language, final wording); sound cue; reference image(s) flagged "reference only"; continuity notes (same room, light direction); brand element and safe-zone note.

## Failure patterns
- Shots chosen before story; pretty clips with no arc.
- Long "before" or feature-first opening; payoff after the drop-off point.
- Text that repeats the picture, or mixed AR/EN lines.
- Logo/CTA covering the reveal; loud promo tone against a quiet-premium brand.
- Raw client footage in the final; unproven statistics quoted as fact.

## Practical evaluation
Rebuild the New Alamein apartment reel ("Empty shell. Sea view. Finished.") as a 15 s story: hook, beat sheet in contract format, AR and EN text versions (separate), sound plan, brand moment, list of beats needing reference photos. Pass if every beat is shot-list-ready and no text line mixes languages.

## Validation rule
UNVALIDATED until the New Alamein beat sheet is turned into a CREATIVE shot list and the Lead reviews it. Using this skill does not validate it. Platform numbers (hook %, retention, watch time) stay "not confirmed" unless a full official URL is cited in the ref files.
