# TSST-LENS-SK-004 Customer / ICP / JTBD

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective

Describe which customers are most relevant, what progress they seek, how they judge success, what triggers action, and what prevents purchase using traceable evidence.

## Triggers

Use for ICP definition, JTBD analysis, customer research synthesis, segmentation, trigger/pain/outcome mapping, or evidence review of a persona.

## Reusable principles

- Use the job executor and sought progress as units of analysis, not fictional demographics.
- Separate functional job, job steps, desired outcomes, emotional/social jobs, purchase triggers, and consumption context.
- Write desired outcomes as measurable directions where evidence allows.
- Segment by meaningful differences in needs, context, behavior, or value, not by arbitrary labels.
- Trace every persona-like statement to research or label it as a hypothesis.
- Keep buyer, user, approver, and beneficiary distinct when they differ.

## Workflow

1. State the decision and available first-party customer evidence.
2. Define the market and candidate job executors.
3. Collect or review interviews, behavioral data, support/sales records, win-loss evidence, and relevant quantitative data.
4. Extract triggers, job statements, steps, desired outcomes, anxieties, alternatives, objections, and buying roles.
5. Code recurring evidence and retain contradictory cases.
6. Form evidence-backed segments and state inclusion, exclusion, and confidence.
7. Build the ICP from fit, need, value, access, and disqualifying criteria.
8. List hypotheses and the research needed to validate them.

## Output contract

- evidence inventory and sample limits;
- job executor and core functional job;
- job map and desired outcomes;
- triggers, pains, alternatives, objections, and buying context;
- ICP criteria and exclusions;
- evidence links, confidence rationale, and unresolved hypotheses.

## Guardrails

- Never invent a persona, quote, pain, objection, or demographic detail.
- Do not treat a sales opinion as customer evidence without labeling it.
- Do not collapse user and buyer roles.
- Do not force all JTBD variants into one methodology without stating the chosen method.
- Do not claim market prevalence from qualitative evidence alone.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Decision and evidence base are explicit.
- [ ] Job executor, buyer, and user roles are distinguished.
- [ ] Jobs and outcomes trace to evidence.
- [ ] Segments have behavioral or contextual distinctions.
- [ ] ICP includes positive and disqualifying criteria.
- [ ] Unsupported items are hypotheses with a validation plan.

## Practical evaluation

Using an evaluator-supplied product and mixed customer evidence, create an ICP and JTBD analysis. The fixture includes conflicting interviews, sales-team assumptions, usage data, and one invented persona detail that must be rejected.

## Common failure modes

- demographic fiction presented as insight;
- pains copied from marketing copy;
- job statement too broad to guide decisions;
- desired outcomes that cannot be assessed;
- qualitative sample used to claim market size;
- buyer and user conflated.
