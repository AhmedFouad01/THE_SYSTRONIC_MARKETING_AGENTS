# TSST-LENS-SK-003 Competitor Intelligence

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective

Produce a comparable view of competitors that explains what is observable, what is inferred, what remains unknown, and how the evidence may affect a decision.

## Triggers

Use for competitor matrices, ad-library reviews, offer and messaging comparisons, channel/funnel observation, or competitive inputs to positioning.

## Reusable principles

- Define the competitive set from the customer's alternatives, not only category labels.
- Use consistent dimensions, geography, dates, and capture procedures.
- Separate observed facts, derived comparisons, and hypotheses.
- Analyze positioning, offer, price, proof, creative, channels, and funnel as connected evidence.
- A visible ad proves presence and content, not targeting, spend, performance, or strategy.
- Archive enough context for another reviewer to reproduce the observation.

## Workflow

1. State the decision, market, audience, time window, and competitor-selection rule.
2. Define comparison dimensions and evidence thresholds before collection.
3. Collect first-party competitor pages, pricing, product documentation, public filings, and official ad-library observations where relevant.
4. Record URL, capture date, geography, status, and exact observed content.
5. Normalize offers, price units, claims, proof, creative patterns, calls to action, channels, and funnel steps.
6. Mark each field `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, or `UNKNOWN`.
7. Identify patterns, gaps, and hypotheses without claiming unseen performance.
8. Connect implications to the stated decision and list validation needs.

## Output contract

- competitor inclusion rationale;
- source log with capture metadata;
- five-competitor comparison matrix;
- observed/inferred/unknown labels;
- screenshots or archived excerpts where permitted;
- implications, hypotheses, and limitations.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce the analysis to SWOT.
- Do not infer targeting, budget, conversion rate, or business performance from Meta Ad Library presence.
- Do not treat missing visible evidence as proof that an activity does not exist.
- Do not copy competitor creative or protected assets.
- Recheck time-sensitive ads and offers before use.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Competitive set and dimensions are justified.
- [ ] Sources, dates, geography, and capture method are recorded.
- [ ] Facts and inferences are visibly separated.
- [ ] Offers and prices use comparable units and conditions.
- [ ] Funnel and channel gaps are labeled unknown when not observable.
- [ ] Decision implications trace to evidence.

## Practical evaluation

Build a matrix for five evaluator-selected competitors using public sources and the Meta Ad Library. Include positioning, offer, pricing, proof, creative, channels, and observable funnel steps. The evaluator seeds one duplicated ad, one expired offer, and one unsupported targeting claim to detect.

## Common failure modes

- competitor list chosen by familiarity;
- screenshot collection without normalized analysis;
- inferred strategy presented as fact;
- old offers mixed with current offers;
- ad presence treated as performance evidence;
- generic SWOT with no decision relevance.
