# TSST-LENS-SK-006 Marketing Planning

Status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Objective

Create a time-bounded plan in which objectives drive strategy, strategy determines channel roles and initiatives, and KPIs and budget logic make progress and tradeoffs reviewable.

## Triggers

Use for 30/60/90-day plans, annual or quarterly marketing plans, go-to-market planning, channel-role design, KPI frameworks, or plan audits.

## Reusable principles

- Tie marketing objectives to explicit business objectives.
- Treat strategy as a set of choices about audience, value, and approach, not a list of activities.
- Give each channel a defined role in the customer journey.
- Define KPIs with formulas, data sources, cadence, owner, target basis, and limitations.
- Make budget logic and assumptions visible; preserve flexibility for evidence-based reallocation.
- Include a learning agenda and decision points, not only a delivery calendar.

## Workflow

1. Confirm business objective, baseline, horizon, constraints, and decision owner.
2. Define measurable marketing objectives and the causal assumptions connecting them to the business objective.
3. Select audiences, positioning, and strategic choices; state what will not be pursued.
4. Assign channel roles and initiatives based on the strategy and evidence.
5. Build a KPI tree and measurement plan with SPARK_AG review where paid media or attribution is involved.
6. Allocate resources using transparent budget logic, dependencies, and scenarios where authorized.
7. Sequence a 90-day roadmap with owners, decision gates, risks, and learning questions.
8. Review coherence from objective through KPI and remove activities that do not serve it.

## Output contract

- business and marketing objectives;
- audience, positioning, strategy, and exclusions;
- channel-role and initiative map;
- KPI dictionary/tree with sources and cadence;
- budget/resource logic and assumptions;
- 90-day roadmap, owners, dependencies, risks, and learning agenda;
- governance and review cadence.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn the plan into a posting calendar.
- Do not invent baselines, targets, budgets, or attribution precision.
- Do not include a channel because it is fashionable or available.
- Do not mix output metrics with business outcomes without a causal explanation.
- Require SPARK_AG review for paid-media measurement and CREATIVE_AG review for creative feasibility.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Objectives are measurable and linked.
- [ ] Strategy contains real choices and exclusions.
- [ ] Each channel has a clear role.
- [ ] KPIs have definitions, formulas, sources, owners, and cadence.
- [ ] Budget logic and assumptions are visible.
- [ ] Roadmap includes decision gates and learning questions.

## Practical evaluation

Build a 90-day plan from an evaluator-supplied business case with incomplete baselines and a fixed budget envelope. The plan must state hypotheses and metrics, refuse to invent missing targets, and distinguish strategy from a content calendar.

## Common failure modes

- activity list with no strategic choice;
- objectives expressed as channels or deliverables;
- KPI names without formulas or sources;
- fixed budget allocations unsupported by evidence;
- no dependencies or decision cadence;
- every audience and channel included.
