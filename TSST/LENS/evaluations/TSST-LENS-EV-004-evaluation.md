# TSST-LENS-SK-004 Evaluation — Customer / ICP / JTBD

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Create an ICP and JTBD analysis from an evaluator-supplied product evidence pack containing interviews, behavior data, support records, and sales assumptions.

## Required deliverables

- evidence inventory and limitations;
- job executor, buyer/user roles, core job, job steps, and desired outcomes;
- triggers, alternatives, objections, and context;
- evidence-backed segments and ICP criteria;
- hypothesis and validation backlog.

## Test conditions

Include contradictory interviews, a non-representative sample, an invented persona detail, and a sales assertion not supported by customer evidence.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score evidence traceability, role separation, behavioral segmentation, outcome clarity, and appropriate confidence.

## Automatic failures

Invented persona fact or quote; qualitative sample used for prevalence; buyer/user conflation; or untraceable pain points.
