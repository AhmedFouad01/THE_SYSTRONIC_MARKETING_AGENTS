# TSST-LENS-SK-006 Evaluation — Marketing Planning

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Create a 90-day marketing plan from an evaluator-supplied business case with a fixed budget envelope and incomplete baselines. Do not execute any initiative.

## Required deliverables

- linked business and marketing objectives;
- audience, positioning, strategic choices, and exclusions;
- channel roles and initiatives;
- KPI dictionary/tree;
- budget logic and assumptions;
- roadmap, owners, dependencies, risks, review cadence, and learning agenda.

## Test conditions

Include one fashionable but strategically irrelevant channel, missing baseline data, conflicting stakeholder priorities, and a paid-media KPI requiring SPARK_AG review.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score objective-strategy coherence, real choices, KPI precision, budget transparency, sequencing, and cross-agent handoff quality.

## Automatic failures

Invented target or baseline; plan reduced to a post calendar; channel included without role; or paid-media recommendation accepted without measurement logic.
