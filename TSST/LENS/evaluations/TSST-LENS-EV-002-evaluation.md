# TSST-LENS-SK-002 Evaluation — Source Validation & Evidence Grading

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Evaluate ten supplied sources against five specific marketing claims and produce an auditable claim-evidence ledger.

## Required deliverables

- source metadata and type;
- criterion-by-criterion assessment;
- claim-specific grade and rationale;
- corroboration/conflict map;
- disposition for each claim;
- missing-evidence plan.

## Test conditions

Include an official outdated page, a recent article based on old data, an opaque vendor study, a strong primary source, conflicting definitions, and three articles repeating one original source.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score claim-specific reasoning, freshness relative to volatility, source independence, methodology review, and appropriate qualification.

## Automatic failures

Global grade with no claim context; duplicated reporting counted as corroboration; unsupported claim accepted; or archived guidance presented as current.
