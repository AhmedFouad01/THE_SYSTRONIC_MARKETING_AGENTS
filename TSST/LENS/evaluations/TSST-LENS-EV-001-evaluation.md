# TSST-LENS-SK-001 Evaluation — Research Planning

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Design a research brief for an evaluator-specified new market. Start from the business decision and produce answerable research questions, methods, evidence requirements, and deliverables.

## Required deliverables

- decision and owner;
- questions, hypotheses, and unknowns;
- scope and definitions;
- evidence/method matrix;
- sample/source-selection logic;
- analysis, QA, timeline, limitations, and stop conditions.

## Test conditions

The fixture includes a short deadline, limited first-party data, two plausible methods, a biased source, and one request that cannot be answered defensibly.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score decision alignment, method fit, scope discipline, feasibility, evidence planning, and explicit uncertainty.

## Automatic failures

Indiscriminate collection plan; invented data access; unqualified promise of representativeness; or facts and hypotheses mixed together.
