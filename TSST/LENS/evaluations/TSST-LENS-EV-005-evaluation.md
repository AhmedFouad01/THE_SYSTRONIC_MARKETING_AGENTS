# TSST-LENS-SK-005 Evaluation — Positioning & Messaging

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Build a positioning canvas and message house from an evaluator-supplied product, customer, and competitor evidence pack.

## Required deliverables

- customer alternatives;
- differentiated capabilities and resulting value;
- best-fit audience and exclusions;
- category choice and tradeoffs;
- positioning statement;
- message pillars, proof, objections, and claim qualifications;
- unresolved tests.

## Test conditions

The fixture includes status quo as an alternative, a feature competitors also possess, one unprovable superlative, and a segment that does not value the claimed differentiator.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score evidence-backed differentiation, component logic, audience fit, category clarity, proof, and claim discipline.

## Automatic failures

Unprovable claim presented as fact; “everyone” target; irrelevant competitor set; or a slogan substituted for positioning.
