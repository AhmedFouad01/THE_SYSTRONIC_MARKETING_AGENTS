# TSST-LENS-SK-003 Evaluation — Competitor Intelligence

Status: `QUEUED`  
Skill status: `UNVALIDATED`

## Task

Create a competitor-intelligence matrix for five evaluator-selected competitors using public first-party sources and Meta Ad Library observations.

## Required deliverables

- selection rationale and comparison dimensions;
- capture/source log;
- normalized matrix covering positioning, offer, price, proof, creative, channels, and observable funnel;
- `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, and `UNKNOWN` labels;
- implications and limitations.

## Test conditions

The fixture includes a duplicated ad, an expired offer, regional price differences, and an unsupported targeting claim.

## Evaluation emphasis

Score comparable collection, fact/inference separation, temporal accuracy, reproducibility, and decision relevance.

## Automatic failures

Targeting, spend, or performance inferred from ad presence; expired data presented as current; copied creative; or missing source/capture metadata.
