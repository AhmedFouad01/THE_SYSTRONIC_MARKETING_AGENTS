# CREATIVE Intake Gate (stage 0)

Version: 0.1 — 2026-10-07. Evidence: `TSST/PILOTS/TSST-PILOT-002_VILL_STRATEGY_BASELINE/` (work became right only after the user answered direction, references, palette, language, first format, photos).

## Rule
No render, and no brief for a direction, until every BLOCKING field is filled or explicitly answered by the user. Missing field = ask (max 6 questions), never guess.

## BLOCKING fields
1. asset id, platform, format and px, placement, safe zones
2. goal and funnel stage; one audience
3. single message, verbatim from the message house
4. POV/angle
5. ONE chosen direction (user has not chosen = no brief)
6. style target: 2-3 references, each with what to take, plus a "must NOT look like" line
7. hero image source: path + rights (or explicit "type-only")
8. exact on-image text per level (hierarchy below)
9. CTA text and channel (or "no CTA")
10. proof asset for any claim
11. composition intent: focal point, text zone, clear zone
12. brand assets in force: logo file, palette, fonts

OPTIONAL: variation axes; alt text/caption; deadline/approver; competitor notes.

## The 6 questions (ask before any render)
1. Which direction? 2. Which references, and what to take from each? 3. Which photo / proof asset?
4. Exact on-image text? 5. Text zone and focal point? 6. Format, placement, CTA channel?

## On-image text hierarchy (LENS delivers the words)
- H1: 3-6 words. Support line: 0-1 line, 6-10 words. CTA: 2-4 words + channel. Callouts: 4 words or fewer.
- Feed 4:5: H1 <=6. Carousel cover: H1 <=6. Inner slide: 12-20 words. Story/reel cover: H1 <=5, top third, outside UI zones.
- Caption text stays off the image. Production state and rights never appear on the art.

## Handover
Filled fields are recorded in the asset brief; unfilled non-blocking fields are listed as `OPEN`.
