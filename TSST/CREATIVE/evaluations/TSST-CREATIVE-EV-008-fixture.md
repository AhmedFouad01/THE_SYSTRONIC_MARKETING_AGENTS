## Brand brief

NABD is a fictional bilingual B2B platform for municipal water-network teams in the Middle East. It helps operations leaders see pressure anomalies, prioritize inspections, and document maintenance decisions before hidden leaks become disruptive failures.

Audience: municipal operations directors, network engineers, and infrastructure-program executives.  
Decision to support: request a technical assessment/demo.  
Approved promise: **Find pressure loss before it becomes water loss.**  
Approved Arabic: **اكتشف هبوط الضغط قبل أن يتحول إلى فاقد مياه.**  
Approved proof statement for the executive slide: **One operational view connects pressure signals, inspection priority, ownership, and the decision trail.**  
Arabic proof: **رؤية تشغيلية واحدة تربط إشارات الضغط وأولوية الفحص والمسؤولية ومسار القرار.**

Brand character: observant, civic-minded, exact, calm under pressure.  
Avoid: generic SaaS gradients, neon cyberpunk, disaster imagery, playful droplets, luxury styling, unsupported savings percentages, fear-based claims, and imitation of an existing water or technology brand.

Required visual idea: make hidden infrastructure legible without turning the work into a literal dashboard screenshot.  
Language: Arabic and English must both be considered; direction and line breaks must be credible.  
Surfaces: social feed ad, 16:9 executive slide, responsive desktop/mobile web hero.

## Production constraints

- Use a typographic `NABD | نبض` lockup created for the lab; do not claim it is a final logo.
- Imagery may be generated for this fictional evaluation and must not imply a real customer site.
- Use only the approved copy above plus neutral labels such as `Pressure signal`, `Inspection priority`, `Owner`, and `Decision trail`.
- CTA may be `Request a technical assessment` / `اطلب تقييماً فنياً`.
- The output must show two or three genuinely different directions, select one, and render all three required channel applications.
