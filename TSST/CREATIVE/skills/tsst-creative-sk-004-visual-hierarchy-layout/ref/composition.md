# Composition principles + interior specifics (UNVALIDATED; distilled from GEM-CR-005, Lead-accepted as craft input)
Every principle has a measurable test. Run it on the actual crop at output size. Tools: `tools/text_space.py`, `tools/contrast.py`, `tools/phone_preview.py`.

## Part 1 - Principles
| Principle | Test | Crop for it | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thirds / phi grid (lines at 33 % or 38 %) | Subject's eye or hero object within ~5 % of frame width of an intersection | Put the subject on one vertical line, the horizon on one horizontal | Dead-centring by default |
| Leading lines | Trace walls, table edges, floor lines: do they converge on the subject? | Start the line at a corner or edge | Lines that carry the eye out of frame |
| Symmetry / one-point | Fold in half: weights equal, verticals parallel to the frame | Centre exactly, 0 deg tilt | 48/52 reads as an error, not intent |
| Framing | Foreground encloses the subject on 2-3 sides | Let frame elements touch the photo edge | Frame brighter than the subject |
| Figure-ground | Heavy blur: subject still a separate blob | Crop out clutter with the subject's luminance/colour | Subject lost in a busy background |
| Visual weight | Split down the middle: one side not overwhelmingly heavier | Leave quiet space opposite a heavy subject | All heavy elements on one side |
| Eye path (Z / F) | Start at the brightest/highest-contrast spot: does the path reach text then logo? | Subject gaze or pointing towards the text zone | Gaze exits the frame |
| Negative space | At least 30-40 % of the frame free of high-contrast detail | Zoom out, or move the crop to include a quiet plane | Filling every pixel |
| Scale cue | Can a viewer estimate size without a caption? | Keep a chair, hand or plant, even cropped | Isolated object that looks tiny or huge |
| Colour/light as focal | Squint: is the brightest/most saturated spot the subject? (SK-008 v0.3: light leads the eye) | Crop out blown windows and lamps that are not the subject | A lamp pulling the eye off a dim subject |

## Part 2 - Interior / product specifics
Numbers marked (default) are common practice, not facts.
- Verticals: walls within 1 deg of parallel to the frame edge, else fail. Keystone fix up to ~15 deg (default); beyond that corners stretch.
- Camera height: mid-room, about 100-120 cm (default). Test: does it read as a seated adult, not a child or a ceiling drone?
- Wide-angle: under ~24 mm equivalent, keep people/round objects in the central ~60 %; test for edge stretch.
- One-point vs two-point: commit to a flat 0 deg wall or a clear 30-45 deg corner; never a "1.5-point".
- Shot set: wide (whole room), medium (sofa + table), detail (material). Test: does the carousel hold all three?
- Vignettes: odd groups (3), varied heights forming a triangle.
- Texture: needs raking side light; front light flattens. Test: can you see the grain/relief?
- Window light: interior bulbs must not cast contradicting shadows or colour temperature against window light.
- Before/after: same position, focal length, height. Test: 50 % overlay, structural walls align.
