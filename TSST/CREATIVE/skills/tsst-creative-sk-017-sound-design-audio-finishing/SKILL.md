# TSST-CREATIVE-SK-017 — Sound Design & Audio Finishing

Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Training installed: 2026-09-24  
Owner: `CREATIVE_AG`

## Objective

Use voice, music, ambience and SFX to create clarity, rhythm and brand feel without masking the message or violating rights.

## Source / provenance

Adobe Premiere official audio/mixing guidance + platform commercial-audio rights rules.

## Required method

1. Prioritize intelligible dialogue/voice; clean obvious noise where tools allow.
2. Use music to establish pace and emotion; duck it under speech rather than competing with it.
3. Add restrained transition/impact/texture SFX only where they reinforce a visual event.
4. Check peaks, perceived loudness consistency and abrupt edit clicks.
5. Confirm music/SFX licensing for the actual target platform and paid/organic use.

## Failure patterns

- music louder than speech
- SFX on every transition
- copyright status assumed from availability
- abrupt audio cuts
- same audio bed pasted across pieces with different pacing

## Practical evaluation

Mix a 15–20s short with voice + music + 3–5 purposeful SFX and deliver a rights/level QA note.

## Validation rule

Package installation and study do not validate the skill. Validation requires actual evidence appropriate to the medium. Render-dependent skills cannot pass on briefs, prompts, HTML previews or unrendered storyboards alone.
