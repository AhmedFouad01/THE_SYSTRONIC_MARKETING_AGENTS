# TSST-CREATIVE-SK-013 — Image Compositing & Retouching

Status: `UNVALIDATED`  
Training installed: 2026-09-24  
Owner: `CREATIVE_AG`

## Objective

Integrate subjects, products and environments so the final composite has credible edges, perspective, light, shadow, tone and material behavior.

## Source / provenance

Adobe Photoshop official masking/layer-mask/compositing guidance; non-destructive editing principles.

## Required method

1. Use non-destructive masks/selections and preserve source assets.
2. Match perspective/scale before effects; match light direction, shadow softness and contact points.
3. Correct local exposure/white balance/color so sources belong in the same scene.
4. Refine edges, hair/product contours and halos at 100% and target size.
5. Use retouching to remove distractions, not to destroy natural texture or product truth.

## Failure patterns

- floating products with no contact shadow
- mismatched lighting/color temperatures
- hard cutout halos
- fake reflections/shadows that contradict geometry
- over-retouched skin or altered product labels

## Practical evaluation

Composite a supplied product/subject into a new scene and pass a 100% edge/light/perspective QA checklist.

## Validation rule

Package installation and study do not validate the skill. Validation requires actual evidence appropriate to the medium. Render-dependent skills cannot pass on briefs, prompts, HTML previews or unrendered storyboards alone.
