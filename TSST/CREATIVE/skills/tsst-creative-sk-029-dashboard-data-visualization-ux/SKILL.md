# TSST-CREATIVE-SK-029 — Dashboard & Data Visualization UX


Status: `UNVALIDATED`
Installed: 2026-10-03
Owner: CREATIVE_AG
Scope: Product UI/UX for the Systronic CRM MVP and future enterprise SaaS interfaces.


## Objective
Design CRM reporting that communicates decisions and trends accurately without dashboard noise, inaccessible color coding or decorative chart overload.


## Trigger
Use for dashboards, pipeline analytics, source/attribution reports, conversion/revenue views and KPI summaries.


## Sources
- https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/data-visualization/chart-anatomy
- https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/data-visualization/legends
- https://atlassian.design/foundations/color-new/data-visualization-color/


## Workflow
1. Start from decisions/questions, not chart types.
2. Use KPI hierarchy and progressive drill-down.
3. Choose chart based on comparison/trend/composition task.
4. Label units, time windows, attribution model and source truth.
5. Prefer direct labels where feasible.
6. Use color semantically and never alone.
7. Provide table/text alternative for important chart data.
8. Design hover, selection and drill-down states.


## Output contract
Metric hierarchy; chart-selection rationale; dashboard layout; interaction/drill-down rules; accessibility and data-truth annotations.


## Guardrails
- No chart without a question it answers.
- Do not mix currencies/outcomes silently.
- No 3D/decorative charts.
- Do not rely on color alone or unexplained jargon.


## Practical evaluation
Redesign M2 reporting to answer five sales/marketing decisions in under one screen-depth each; pass data-truth and accessibility review.
