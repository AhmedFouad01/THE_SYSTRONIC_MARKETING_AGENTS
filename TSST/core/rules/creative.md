# Rules pack — design, images, video, external production

Load for studio, image, video or external-tool production. Core rules still apply.
Original wording: `TSST/decisions/rules-history.md`. Production standard: `TSST/CREATIVE/AGENT.md`.

- **R36 Owner drafts are the floor.** Raise Ahmed's draft; keep what he accepted; ask before
  dropping any element.
- **Brand rules** Brand-specific owner rules (e.g. VILL photo pairing, kitchen colour mood, bold cinematic rounds) live in `TSST/brands/<id>/rules.md`; load them with this pack.
- **R37 Measure brand facts.** Before saying something matches a brand, measure it against current
  approved assets (hex/hue/font) and name what was measured.
- **R38 AI shots, not raw slideshows.** Client raw media is reference only; plan video per shot
  (reference → AI still → AI video → assembly); confirm the pipeline with Ahmed first. CREATIVE + MOTION.
- **R31 Video fits the package.** Client video: one-pass production, one credit quote, max one
  re-roll; shot-by-shot QA only for big budgets or training rounds.
- **R41 External production.** Method: `TSST/context/external-production.md`. Confirm the brief with
  Ahmed; Lead directs and reviews; Ahmed bridges a bounded English request to the external tool.
  Stage 1 = images + comparison report only; stage 2 (into the Delivery System) needs a separate OK.
  No automatic rerolls, unrestricted writes or publication.
- **Logos.** Approved logos/brand marks are preserved or composited, never regenerated.
- **PREVIS.** HTML layouts, prompts, briefs and storyboards are `PREVIS`; final assets need render QA
  through `TSST/CREATIVE/FINAL_ASSET_GATE.md`. Video/motion belongs to MOTION (`TSST/MOTION/AGENT.md`);
  CREATIVE does its final brand check.
- **R63 Photos first, temporary = fast** (owner 2026-11-12): a page that needs imagery starts with a one-line ask for Ahmed's
  best photos, not a social-media scrape. When he calls something temporary: one version built in the session, a screenshot
  within the hour, no multi-direction workflows.
