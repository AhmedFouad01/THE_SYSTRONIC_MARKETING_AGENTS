# Creative 02 — تحت الشدّ / Under Tension

Phase: Tease, second installment. Working assumption pending campaign-phase preference; this is a new creative for review, not an approved reveal.

## Objective
Extend intrigue by revealing signs of strength through physical tension while withholding the new identity.

## Arabic headline
القوة ليها ملامح.

## Arabic support
قريب… الصورة هتكمل.

## Visual concept
Black technical textile stretched over a concealed asymmetric angular volume. Raking white light reveals weave and tensile lines. A tiny orange seam reinforcement is physical material rather than illumination.

## Composition
Elevated wider viewpoint. Tension travels across the upper image from left to right, with the seam at the point of concentrated force. Type is lower-right. No glowing gap, steel-block framing or top-left headline from Creative 01 is reused.

## Why it works
The silhouette suggests strength before revealing its source. The material adds an athletic association, while hard geometry, black values and restrained orange connect to the ANVIL world. It advances curiosity through concealment and tension rather than repeating the first composition.

## Typography and deliverables
Actual local Cairo Bold 700 and Regular 400 fonts, verified from font metadata. Genuine shaped text composited onto built-in imagegen photographic plate. 1080 × 1350 final plus 360 × 450 feed-size QA. This rendered proposal is not a product or textile-quality claim. No logo or identity redesign.

## Final image-generation prompt
Use case: ads-marketing. Create one premium photoreal art-directed social campaign photographic plate, portrait 4:5, for the ANVIL SUPPS teaser campaign. Creative02 concept: STRENGTH TAKES SHAPE. Must be visually compelling WITHOUT typography. No words or logo.

A single mysterious angular sculptural volume is concealed under a taut black high-performance technical textile membrane. This is a deliberately engineered, tensioned textile sculpture, NOT a draped blanket or a covered product. One long sharp asymmetric ridge rises from upper-left toward the center-right; at its peak the cloth is stretched drum-tight, revealing a precisely machined hard silhouette beneath without exposing what the object is. Multiple subtle tensile pull-lines radiate from that ridge toward cropped anchor points outside the upper and side edges, creating beautiful large triangular planes of darkness. Cloth is matte dense fine woven technical fabric, plausible fine threads in highlights, no glossy plastic or leather. Make the hidden weight and physical tensile forces believable. One short, precision orange woven reinforcement tab, ANVIL Orange #FF6A00, integrated into a seam near the highest strain point at approximately x65%,y38%; it is a small actual material detail, not a light source or decorative luminous streak.

Composition is an elevated three-quarter wide studio view, materially different from a close-up of steel blocks: a sculptural textile landscape suspended under tension across the UPPER TWO THIRDS of the image, with one defined ridge and enough surrounding darkness to understand its form. Strong diagonal movement left to right. Lower third is calm uninterrupted almost-black space, particularly bottom-right for later genuine Arabic typography. Keep outer edges cropped with intention. Deep negative background no visible set or floor horizon. Main sculptural ridge large enough to be instantly understood at phone-feed size. Actual sophisticated photographic depth, restrained editorial sports-luxury campaign attitude. No anvil prop. No product shape, no bottle, no packaging, no human.

Lighting: one expertly flagged cool white raking softbox, sculpting an elegant thin highlight along the tense ridge and grazing the fabric's woven texture. Falloff into soft charcoal half-tones and near-black, with convincing occlusion under the lifted membrane. Black #080808 / charcoal / white highlights, orange under1% of total frame. Avoid weak flat darkness; form must read instantly, detailed physical texture and incredible material realism. No orange illumination, no crack between two blocks, no metal-block composition, no front-facing poster layout, no smoke, sparks, fire, particles, sci-fi, neon, chrome, excess wrinkles, grunge, background wallpaper pattern, or text. Deliver one exquisite finished photographic background suitable for a premium athletic brand campaign.
