## Strategy → visual idea

**اسم جديد. امتداد لنفس الحكاية. / A new name. The story continues.** LENS strategy preserves recognition before building ANVIL memory. The visual mechanism is **One continuous cut / خط واحد بيكمل**: the same horizontal baseline carries Shark X Store, the transition statement, and ANVIL SUPPS. A restrained A-tip edge moves across the composition as an editorial wipe. The old name remains legible long enough to connect the two. Never destroy, burn, cross out or mechanically morph either logo.

FACT: the user explicitly identifies Shark X Store → ANVIL SUPPS and supplies the continuity message. FACT: all eight supplied VI_ANVIL boards were inspected read-only on 2026-09-22. ASSUMPTION: Egyptian Arabic is the primary voice; English brand spelling remains literal. HYPOTHESIS: a direct old/new bridge at the beginning reduces first-exposure confusion. RECOMMENDATION: use that bridge in every paid asset and reveal/reinforce pieces, with the deliberate D7 story F01 response-before-answer exception documented in the production pack; teaser names Shark without disclosing ANVIL prematurely.

## Working system for the designer

- Background off-black; white primary text; concrete gray secondary text. Steel gray only for nonessential dividers, never small body text on black. Orange is a thin progress edge/A-tip accent, not full background. Board ratio is roughly 2% orange: treat as restraint guidance, not a license to hide text under a huge triangle.
- Use the untouched supplied master logo. Master vectors/transparent logo assets are NOT yet verified available; board screenshots are reference only. Do not trace or regenerate geometry. Until production asset intake, mark logo layers `OFFICIAL_MASTER_REQUIRED`; ordinary text spelling ANVIL SUPPS is permitted as text, never represented as the official logo.
- Primary Arabic title Cairo Bold 76 px / 99 px line height on 1080-wide canvas; supporting Cairo Regular 44/62; small labels Cairo Medium 36/50. English display Monument Extended Bold 66/80; English body Inter Regular 38/52. Long brand bridge uses Inter Semibold 40/52, two separated name boxes if necessary. These are starting sizes, not proof of readability; shorten lines rather than squeeze/stretch. Never artificially condense Arabic.
- Arabic paragraphs RTL/right aligned; separate LTR text boxes for literal brand names. Directional relationship uses three vertically stacked boxes (Shark X Store / بقى اسمه / ANVIL SUPPS), avoiding ambiguous RTL arrows. English reference is Shark X Store → ANVIL SUPPS. No tracking added to Arabic; keep connected glyph shaping. Maximum two headline lines.
- 1080×1920 vertical working safe region: x=96–900, y=280–1400. Subtitle block y=1190–1380, maximum two lines. Keep name bridge in x=120–880, y=330–620. This conservative internal starting template is NOT a guaranteed platform safe zone; editor must inspect each actual placement preview before release.
- 1080×1350 feed: x=90–990, y=110–1210, 12-column grid, 24 px gutters. Name bridge upper third, primary visual middle third, CTA bottom third. Minimum 48 px vertical gap between text blocks. Separate layout, never center-crop the vertical video blindly.
- Stories: 1080×1920, same protected text region. Three separate 6-second cards each day; provide static PNG plus timed video wrapper only if desired by publisher. No dependence on auto-advance timing for comprehension.
- Photos: keep faces/products outside text; low-key side lighting, preserved natural skin texture, neutral blacks, restrained highlights. No physique transformation, medicinal claim, fake customer quote or counterfeit label.
- Motion: single clean 8-frame wipe at 30 fps, straight path, no elastic bounce, no strobe, no glitch, no logo warping. Cut on real action where footage exists. Reveal ANVIL already in the first two seconds on D3; cinematic pacing cannot delay comprehension. End cards ≥3 seconds.
- Sound: original/licensed low industrial percussion and recorded room tone. Voice clear, music below speech; normalize consistently, avoid clipping. Every message understandable muted. Do not assume organic music rights cover ads or cross-platform usage.

## Phase treatment

TEASE: visible Shark name, narrow unfinished orange edge, short copy suggesting continuation; no disappearance/closure imagery. REVEAL: both names immediately, full white ANVIL reverse master on quiet field, same baseline resolves. REINFORCE: ANVIL dominates; small clear transition bridge remains. Repetition of name and visual device builds memory without repeated sales messages.
