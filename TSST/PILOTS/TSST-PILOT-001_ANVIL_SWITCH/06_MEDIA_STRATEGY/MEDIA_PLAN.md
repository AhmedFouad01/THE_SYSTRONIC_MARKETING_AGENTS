## Why not 1,100 / 400?

Official TikTok Ads Manager guidance updated July 2026 requires campaign daily/lifetime budgets above USD 50 and ad-group daily budgets above USD 20; lifetime ad-group minimum depends on scheduled days. A seven-day ad group therefore requires roughly USD 140 at the documented daily floor before exchange/currency checks. No current USD/EGP rate or localized account minimum was verified, so this is **not** an exact EGP conversion or account rejection claim. The suggested 400 EGP cannot responsibly be treated as a verified executable seven-day allocation against these USD requirements. With so little total funding, protecting one useful media pool is preferable. Do not use Promote, boosts or Spark Ads as assumed minimum/policy workarounds. TikTok Egypt healthcare supplement advertising is conditional on local regulatory documentation and 18+ targeting; this pilot has no verified documentation. Brand-led content is not a policy exemption.

TikTok architecture for this pilot: **0 paid campaigns / 0 paid ad groups / 0 paid ads**. Seven native organic cores (SW-D01-C01-TT through SW-D07-C01-TT) provide recognition and comments for learning; no claim of paid comparability. A future paid Reach proposal would require a separately approved budget, official local eligibility, adult audience, one ad group and rights-cleared native creative. It is outside this allocation.
