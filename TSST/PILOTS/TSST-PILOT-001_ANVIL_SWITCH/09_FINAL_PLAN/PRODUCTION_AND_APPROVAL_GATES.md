# Production and approval gates / الاعتماد والتنفيذ

This is a production-ready planning package. It does not contain finished ad videos or a certification that unseen final assets comply with the brand. No external action is authorized by this file.

## Reviewable decisions

User review covers the transition idea, Egyptian-Arabic copy direction, seven-day inventory and recommended 1,500 EGP Meta-only paid allocation. TikTok stays in the organic content plan. Approving the plan alone is not an instruction to launch or spend; the user’s execution gate explicitly reserves those actions.

## Dependency-led production sequence

| Step | Owner | Input and output | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Direction approval | User through Lead | Final plan and scripts | Requested changes recorded; direction approved |
| 2. Asset preflight | CREATIVE + client asset owner | Original logo files, licensed fonts, archive footage, talent/music rights | Source files usable and rights confirmed; use prescribed fallback for absent real footage |
| 3. Channel continuity | Lead + client channel owner | Confirmed old/current account names, new handle and service geography | Profile paths verified; no unsupported promises about unchanged operations |
| 4. Batch production | CREATIVE + internal designer/editor | Seven Meta cores, seven TikTok edits, seven three-frame Story sets | Editable timeline/layout files, linked assets and exports delivered; reuse established templates |
| 5. Asset QA | CREATIVE, then LENS and SPARK | Actual final exports | Visual identity, Arabic typography, transition clarity, subtitles, crop/safe zones, audio and placement previews passed |
| 6. Media preflight | SPARK after explicit account-access authorization | Available objective, budget controls, policy eligibility and account billing settings | Selected objective/placements supported; schedule and spending ceiling enforceable; category eligibility resolved |
| 7. Release instruction | User through Lead | Final approved exports + preflight record | Explicit publication/launch/spend authority for exact dates, channels and limit |
| 8. Observation | SPARK + LENS after launch authorization | Daily native exports and coded transition responses | Report directional findings with missing data and attribution limitations |

Production capacity is unconfirmed. The plan uses a common type/layout system and one small real-footage session rather than seven unrelated shoots. If internal capacity cannot deliver the full inventory, Lead must propose a concrete reduction for user approval; no silent deletion of required outputs.

## Risk register

Ratings are qualitative Lead judgments, not measured probabilities.

| Risk | Likelihood / impact | Detection | Mitigation and owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tease is interpreted as closure | Medium / high | Closure or unrelated-business questions | Shark remains identifiable during tease; short two-day suspense; explicit mapping from D3. LENS owns copy correction |
| New viewer sees reinforce without reveal | High / high | Standalone comprehension check | Repeat old→new mapping in every D3–7 entry asset. CREATIVE/LENS |
| Premium visuals imply unsupported product superiority | Medium / high | Claims review | Restrict stronger language to identity/new chapter; no efficacy, authenticity certification or service-continuity claim without proof. Lead |
| TikTok paid minimum/policy incompatibility | High / high for suggested split | Official-source review and later account preflight | TikTok organic; no speculative paid workaround. SPARK |
| Missing logo/font/rights or real footage | Medium / high | Asset preflight | Original logo only, approved licensed fonts, prescribed text/texture fallbacks; hold any unsupported testimonial. CREATIVE |
| Limited budget yields sparse response | High / medium | Counts and denominators visible | Report insufficient evidence honestly; no brand lift, causal winner or long-term benchmark claims. SPARK/LENS |
| Spend pacing differs from plan | Medium / high | Authorized operator checks actual + committed spend | Enforce total cap; reallocate only unspent budget within approved rules; never catch up by exceeding ceiling. SPARK |
| Geography/account migration assumption wrong | Unknown / high | Channel owner preflight | Confirm service geography and exact handles before activation; no invented URL or audience pool. Lead |
| Supplement-category ad restriction | Unknown / high | Current policy and later account/category review | Brand-only execution does not guarantee exemption; hold paid activation if eligibility unresolved. SPARK |

Review the complete plan and recommend edits or approve its direction. Production rendering, final asset QA and execution preflight remain subsequent work; current package readiness refers only to user review.
