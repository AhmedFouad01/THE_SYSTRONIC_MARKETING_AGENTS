## 1. Essence
**Idea:** VILL explains every design decision, so the home you approve is the home you get. [S§2 tone/pillars; BL character: precise structure, calm truthful interiors]
Wording is a HYPOTHESIS-level value statement (gap between drawing and finished work, R); it is shown as a question or a labelled concept until proof exists.

| Trait | It means | It is not |
|---|---|---|
| Precise | Short sentences, a named detail, a stated reason | Technical showing-off; invented figures |
| Calm | Plain, reassuring, no pressure; says what is unknown | Hype, urgency, "luxury", emotional drama |
| Accountable | Names scope, exclusions, next step, who answers | Absolute guarantees, fixed price, delivery promises (S§2) |

## 2. Message house
**Core promise (working, S§2):** One vision. One scope. One accountable team. Used only as a qualified line; "accountable" needs delivery proof.
**Roof for kitchen entry:** a kitchen that belongs to the whole home; whole-home is an optional next step, never a push (S§3).

| Pillar | Qualified message (S§2) | Proof needed first |
|---|---|---|
| Design intelligence | «التصميم يبدأ من استخدامك للمساحة» | Approved plan, constraint, choice, result |
| Execution control + detail | «من قرار التصميم لتفاصيل التنفيذ» | Dated site log / QC check / handover record |
| Transparency + accountability | «نطاق واضح وخطوات قابلة للمراجعة» | BOQ, exclusions, change control, named owner |

**Proof types allowed:** plan + explained constraint; dated site log; QC/handover record; BOQ sample (anonymised); authorised testimonial/review; project photo with permission. A render is called a render; a "before/after" needs both sides real.
**Rule:** no proof line without project + source + date + permission. Otherwise omit it or mark `CONCEPT`. Never: fixed price, guaranteed delivery, warranty, "in-house team", fake review, invented number (S§2).

## 3. Voice and tone by context
Constant voice: restrained, architectural, precise, reassuring (S§2). Only warmth and length change.

| Context | Register | Length / shape | Tone dial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social organic | EG | One decision per post; reason in line 2 | Teaching, warm |
| Paid ad | EG (MSA for headline if multi-market) | Hook + one benefit + one CTA | Direct, never urgent |
| WhatsApp reply | EG, same-hour human | 2-3 lines; ask one question | Personal, brief |
| Proposal / quote | Simplified MSA | Scope, exclusions, terms stated plainly | Formal, exact |
| Site | EG headlines, MSA body/terms | Scannable; proof next to claim | Calm, explanatory |

## 4. Language rules
- **Pairing (fixes "separated languages", R):** Arabic leads. Each piece has ONE Arabic line and ONE English line that carry the same idea, grouped together, Arabic first, English smaller. English is a rendering of the Arabic, not a second slogan, and never a separate tagline ("LIVE. BETTER.").
- **Register:** one per piece. EG for ads, captions, WhatsApp; MSA for proposals, terms, legal-ish text. No Franco-Arabic.
- **Loanwords allowed:** ديتيل، سايت، رندر، 3D، واتساب (keep مطبخ, not كيتشن). Keep: BOQ and VILL in Latin. Replace: «تقييم» alone with «نبدأ بسؤالك / مراجعة مشروعك». One spelling per loanword.
- **Numerals:** Western digits (0-9) in all pieces: BL assumption, user decision OPEN. Never mix systems in one price. Unit stays with number (e.g. `60 سم`, `3 غرف`).
- **Bidi:** Arabic RTL, right-aligned; Latin names, URLs, numbers+units isolated (`bdi dir=ltr`); do not end an Arabic line with Latin + punctuation; hashtags supplied separately; no tatweel, no tracking on Arabic (BL). Test in the real platform.
- **Gender:** address the reader in neutral/plural forms where possible (اسألنا، ابعتلنا); avoid gendered adjectives about the client.
- **Spelling sheet:**

| Write | Not |
|---|---|
| اسأل / اسألنا (imperative, hamza on alef) | أسأل (that is "I ask"; found in carousel-02) |
| ابعتلنا / ابدأ | إبدأ |
| إزاي، ليه، دلوقتي، كده | لماذا، الآن in EG pieces |
| التفاصيل (with ال) | تفاصيل تخدم حياتك (generic, R) |
| اللي | الذي in EG pieces |

## 5. Headline and CTA system
Headlines name a decision or a question; never a slogan. ("ONE HOME. ONE VISION." retired as generic, R.)

| # | Pattern | AR (EG) | EN | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reason | كل سنتي ليه سبب. | Every centimetre has a reason. | Detail + stated reason |
| 2 | Question | ليه الدرج بيفتح كده؟ | Why does this drawer open this way? | One real detail |
| 3 | Contrast | مطبخ مش في طريقك. | A kitchen that stays out of your way. | Concept only |
| 4 | Process | من الرسمة للتركيب، خطوة خطوة. | From drawing to install, step by step. | Plan + log |
| 5 | Scope | إيه اللي داخل في النطاق وإيه اللي لأ؟ | What is in scope, what is not? | BOQ sample |
| 6 | Direct offer | ابدأ مطبخك بسؤال واحد. | Start your kitchen with one question. | Live channel |

**CTA by stage** (channel TBC; no CTA names a channel until approved):
- Awareness: احفظ البوست / شوف التفصيلة · Save this / See the detail.
- Consideration: شوف إزاي بنشتغل · See how we work.
- Intent (kitchen): ابدأ مراجعة مطبخك · Start your kitchen review.
- Qualification: ابعتلنا المساحة وميعاد التسليم · Send us the area and handover date.
- Proposal: راجع النطاق معانا · Review the scope with us.
- Post-handover: شاركنا رأيك (only with permission) · Share your review.
No "now", "limited", "last chance", discounts or prices (not authorised).

## 6. Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| «ليه الدرج بيفتح كده؟ عشان الإيد تعدّي من غير ما تخبط.» (explain, concept) | «تفاصيل تخدم حياتك» (generic) |
| «ابعتلنا مساحتك وميعاد التسليم.» | «اسأل عن الحركة والتخزين وعلاقة الخامات» (a to-do list) |
| «نبدأ بمراجعة مشروعك.» | «ابدأ بتقييم مشروعك» (reads like a form) |
| One AR line + same-meaning EN line | Unrelated EN tagline beside AR |
| "Render" labelled as render | Passing a render off as a finished project |
| «النطاق مكتوب، وأي تعديل بيتوافق عليه.» (only if change control is approved) | «تسليم مضمون في ميعاده» |
| State the unknown: «السعر بيتحدد بعد المعاينة.» | Any number, price, or "starting from" |
| Teaching verb: شوف، اسأل، ابعت | Pressure: لا تفوّت، آخر فرصة |
| One CTA per piece | Internal notes on public posts (e.g. "optional next step") |
| «مطبخك جزء من بيتك» | Luxury hype: «فخامة لا مثيل لها» |
