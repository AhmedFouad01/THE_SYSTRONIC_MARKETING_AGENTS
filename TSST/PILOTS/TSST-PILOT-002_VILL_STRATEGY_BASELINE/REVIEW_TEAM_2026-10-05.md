## CREATIVE DIRECTION for the redo (SK-008 method: 3 directions, 1 selection)
Shared mechanism ("the Cut"): the logo's ~19 deg lean and its navy cuts are the grid. Navy slabs slice the frame at exactly that angle; the orange wedge (V cut) is the only orange object and is used as a *pointer/marker*, once per piece. Photo is full-bleed or large; type sits on a navy slab, never floating on photo.

**A. "اللي اتصمم هو اللي اتركّب" — Drawn = Built.** Insight (HYPOTHESIS): owners fear the gap between the rendering/drawing and what is installed. Visual: one photo split along the logo angle: one side as dimensioned line drawing (navy lines/orange dimensions) of the same joinery/kitchen, other side the built result. Needs a real plan + real photo pair (proof pillar). AR: «اللي في الرسمة.. هو اللي في بيتك.» · «من المسطرة للتركيب.» · «شوف الرسمة جنب الشغل.» EN: "Drawn. Then built." (qualified: only on projects with documented plan/handover).
**B. "كل سنتي ليه سبب" — Every centimetre has a reason.** Insight: design intelligence is invisible unless explained. Visual: one macro detail (joinery edge, handle clearance, socket height) filling the frame, 2-3 orange wedge markers with numbered navy callouts that state the *reason*. Carousel: each slide = one decision. AR: «كل سنتي ليه سبب.» · «ليه الدرج بيفتح كده؟» · «التفصيلة اللي مش بتلاحظها.. هي اللي بتفرق.» EN: "Every centimetre has a reason." Proof: constraint + choice + result per slide.
**C. "مطبخ مش في طريقك" — A kitchen that stays out of your way.** Insight: kitchens are judged at 7 am, not at the handover photo; ties to the kitchen-first paid entry (v0.2 §3). Visual: tall full-bleed kitchen in morning light, huge Arabic headline on a slab cut at the logo angle; small time stamp "٧:٠٠ ص". Variants differ by moment (coffee, school bags, dinner). AR: «مطبخ مش في طريقك.» · «الصبح مش وقت تدوّر على حاجة.» · «ابدأ بالسؤال: بتطبخ إزاي؟» CTA AR: «ابعتلنا على واتساب نبدأ من سؤالك.» EN: "A kitchen that stays out of your way."
**Selection recommendation:** B as the organic system (carries Design/Execution/Transparency pillars with proof, repeatable, brand-ownable) + C as the kitchen paid entry (v0.2 containment: same grammar). A as the hero concept once a real plan-vs-built pair exists. User decision needed on which to develop first.

## Art-direction rules
- Arabic first and largest; Egyptian conversational but precise; <= 6 words headline; one idea per asset; English is a short support line (not a translation, not interleaved), parity not required.
- Type: heavy Arabic display (Cairo 800-900; test, do not fake-bold); Latin display must be *wide/extended* heavy italic to match the logo (candidates to test with OFL licence: Archivo Expanded/ExtraExpanded Italic, Montserrat ExtraBold Italic); Barlow Condensed only for small labels if at all. SK-005 test on real strings at 360 px.
- Angle constant = measure from Logo.png (~19 deg) and reuse; no free-hand skews. Orange <= 10%, wedge/CTA only; never white text on orange (v1.0 table stays).
- Photography: native >= 2400 px long side, verticals corrected, natural window light, styled/decluttered (no AC units, towels, cables), 1 hero subject; detail macros for B; no screenshots, no upscaling, no AI-generated "projects"; renders labelled as renders.
- Variation (at least two per set): subject, crop scale, slab position/side, type size, light/dark canvas. Reel/Story = recomposed 9:16 with hook in top-third; keep logo inside safe zone.
- No in-art production labels; hand-off record carries state/rights. Every asset has a real CTA with a channel.
- QA: SK-010 at 360 px phone + feed crop; log defects with severity; then CREATIVE_AG internal QA, then user.
