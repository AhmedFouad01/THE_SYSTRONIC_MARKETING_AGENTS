## 3. Offer architecture وconversion journey

**RECOMMENDATION:** الشركة تقدم full interior design / fit-out كنطاق جامع. مسارات عرض مقترحة: design scope، execution/fit-out scope، integrated whole-home scope؛ كل quote يحدد ما يشمله وما يستثنيه، ownership، timeline، payment/change terms. توافر design-only تجاريًا OPEN، وليس خدمة مؤكدة.

**FACT — stored proposal:** three kitchen packages، 4–6 signature design families وmaterial matrix مقترحات قائمة، لا أسعار/خدمات معتمدة. [S2: `01_CURRENT_STATUS/CURRENT_STATUS.md` §5] **RECOMMENDATION:** احتفظ بالـ3 packages كهيكل مبدئي للمقارنة، differentiated by documented material/spec/service choices؛ أسماءها وأسعارها وتوافرها تنتظر costing واعتماد operations. Estimate range لا يُعرض قبل قاعدة تسعير واقعية؛ عند غيابها، CTA = Project Assessment.

**RECOMMENDATION — kitchen paid entry & creative containment:** الإعلان → dedicated kitchen page → kitchen assessment → measurement → kitchen proposal؛ لا نحول kitchen click إلى generic whole-home page. الإعلان والصفحة يحتفظان بنفس VILL masterbrand mark، Arabic/Latin typography، palette، grid، material/detail photography والنبرة المعتمدة لاحقًا في Brand Look. Hero/CTA يظلان kitchen-specific؛ contextual cue مقترح: “VILL Studio · Interior Design & Fit-out” مع وصف المطبخ كجزء من البيت. Whole-home work يظهر كامتداد اختياري بعد الرسالة الأساسية؛ لا kitchen sub-brand أو لغة بصرية منفصلة. CREATIVE يقارن ad/page/assessment معًا قبل التنفيذ؛ الهوية النهائية تنتظر C7-VILL-04/05 واعتماد المستخدم. Upgrade: سجّل احتياج الغرف الأخرى، ثم اقترح dressing/storage أو whole-home بموافقة العميل وcapacity review؛ expansion ليست عميلًا جديدًا.

**RECOMMENDATION — journey:** Ad/Search/organic/referral → relevant service page → Project Assessment → qualification → estimate where supportable → confirmed site visit/measurement → 3D/proposal with scope → signed contract → Deposit Pending → cleared deposit + operations release → execution → handover proof → review/referral/optional expansion. Signed value، collected cash وrecognized revenue ثلاث قيم مختلفة؛ لا نساوي بينها.

**RECOMMENDATION — sales handling:** intake fields = location، property/service type، area، budget band، handover date، desired timeline، contact preference؛ collect only needed data. Qualified = serviceability + feasible scope/timing + budget fit to a real estimate basis + decision contact + available delivery capacity. Unknown fields = Needs info، not automatically qualified. Sales owner assigns next action/date; proposed business-hours first-human-response SLA ≤30 minutes is a **HYPOTHESIS** to approve against staffing, not observed performance.

## 6. CRM،event map وmeasurement

**RECOMMENDATION — roles to assign:** Sales: stages؛ Operations: serviceability/capacity/site/handover؛ Finance: contract/cash/cost/GP؛ SPARK: measurement/QA؛ Lead: weekly decisions. Staffing unconfirmed.

**RECOMMENDATION — stages:** New → Contacted → Needs info / Qualified → Visit scheduled → Visit completed → Proposal sent → Negotiation → Contract Signed → Deposit Pending → Deposit Received → In execution → Handed over. Lost/Disqualified have reason + timestamp؛ no answer remains pending before a defined follow-up policy. Store stage history; do not overwrite timestamps. Duplicate leads merge under one contact while distinct project opportunities remain separate.

**RECOMMENDATION — payment buffer:** Deposit Pending begins when the executed contract is verified and the required mobilization payment is not yet cleared. Store contract_id، required deposit amount، due_date from signed terms، collected/cleared amount، payment status and follow-up owner. Partial/unverified transfers remain pending؛ overdue = flagged follow-up/hold, not automatic Lost. Deposit Received requires Finance confirmation that the required contractual deposit is cleared. Operations separately approves start against capacity/material/cash commitments؛ signed contract alone cannot release execution.
