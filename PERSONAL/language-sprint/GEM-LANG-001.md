## Learner
- Adult, native Egyptian Arabic speaker, owns a small marketing agency.
- English: lower-intermediate (understands simple sentences, weak when speaking fast in meetings).
- French: beginner / close to zero.
- Goal for BOTH: work — meetings, client calls, introducing the agency, small talk, numbers/prices/dates, short emails and chat messages.
- Time: exactly 2 hours per day. No apps to build — this is a study program.

## Schedule
- Days 1–7: English. Days 8–14: French. Day 15: mixed review + final self-test for both.
- Start with one short paragraph: what is a REALISTIC result after 7 days for each language (be honest — no "fluent in a week").

## Every day must contain (same fixed structure)
1. **Timetable of the 2 hours** in blocks, e.g. 20 min words · 25 min video · 30 min listening + shadowing · 25 min speaking · 20 min review/test. Adjust blocks per day if useful, total = 120 min.
2. **Words & phrases (20–30 per day)** grouped by a work topic of the day. Table columns: term · Arabic meaning · pronunciation written in Arabic letters · example sentence from a meeting. French: add gender (m/f) for nouns.
3. **Videos (1–2 per day)**: real YouTube links, free, watchable from Egypt. Give title · channel · length · which minutes to watch · what to focus on. Prefer channels made for learners and business/meeting language.
4. **Listening (audio)**: one podcast episode or audio lesson link per day + a shadowing instruction (listen → pause → repeat aloud). Also say how to hear any single word (e.g. Google Translate speaker, Forvo).
5. **Speaking task**: one role-play script for the day (e.g. "introduce your agency to a new client") + a ready prompt the learner can paste into a voice AI (Gemini Live / ChatGPT voice) so it plays the client and corrects him.
6. **End-of-day mini test**: 10 quick questions (answers at the end of the document).

## Suggested daily topics (improve if you have a better order)
English: 1 introductions & the agency · 2 meeting phrases (agree/disagree/interrupt politely) · 3 numbers, prices, dates, deadlines · 4 presenting a campaign/results · 5 client calls & clarifying · 6 emails & WhatsApp messages · 7 full mock meeting.
French: 8 sounds + greetings + introducing yourself · 9 numbers, prices, dates · 10 the agency & services · 11 meeting basics (polite phrases, asking to repeat/slow down) · 12 calls & scheduling · 13 short messages/emails · 14 full mock meeting (simple level).
